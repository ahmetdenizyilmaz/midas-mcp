- The PTA plant is real and working: Q1 2026 EBITDA +282% YoY and a swing back to a ₺1.72bn quarterly net profit after a ₺11.88bn loss. This is a leveraged option on an operating inflection that has actually started.

**Bear case**
- Net debt ₺138bn against ~₺9.5bn TTM FAVÖK = **14.5x**, financed in a 37% policy-rate regime. That is the entire investment case in one ratio, and it is the reason JCR cut the rating two notches to A− (tr) on 2026-07-29.
