## Bear case

- **Operations now consume cash.** Q1 2026 EBITDA of **−₺151.4M** (vs +₺556.4M a year earlier) against ₺3,722M of short-term financial loans and ₺212M of cash, at a 37% policy rate. Negative interest coverage. That is a refinancing problem, not an earnings problem.

Real revenue is simultaneously down ~33% YoY — the sales that remain are being financed, not collected.

There is no valuation cushion and no trend support — the price is being held up by headline flow, which is exactly the flow that priced it 71% lower from ₺11.89 in the first place.

## Verdict

QUAGR sits in the worst quadrant of the Q/P map: **low quality (Q 32) at an unattractive price (P 21)** — the rare case where a −71% drawdown has *not* created value, because intrinsic value fell faster than the share price. My base intrinsic value is **₺2.25** against a ₺3.41 market price, a **52% premium**, and the company is now burning at the EBITDA line while its controlling shareholder faces criminal proceedings and receivable days have stretched to eight months. The 0.9× book multiple is the trap: it looks like a discount only until you note that peers trade at 0.5-0.7× and that ₺6.18bn of the book is receivables of uncertain quality.

This is not a horizon problem that patience solves — it is a solvency-and-governance problem that requires specific events. **For any holder, the measured path is trimming into strength at ₺3.63 and ₺4.07, never a forced exit into the ₺3.35 floor**; a close below ₺3.13 would mark structural failure of the only supportive feature on the chart. For anyone not holding, this is a watchlist item, not a position, and the trigger is arithmetic rather than narrative.

**What would change the stance:** a single quarter of positive EBITDA (first check: the H1 2026 filing due ~2026-08-19), receivable days back under 200, and a legal resolution that leaves no company-level liability. Any two of those would restore roughly **+0.15 of R** and lift Q into the 40s, moving FINAL toward the low-30s/Speculative band; all three, combined with a reclaim of the ₺3.81 SMA50 on above-average volume, would put it back in the Hold conversation. Until then, the tape and the accounts are pointing the same way.
