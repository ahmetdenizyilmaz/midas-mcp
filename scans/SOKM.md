## Bull case
- **Cheapest solvent name in the sector by a wide margin:** 0.79× book and 0.11× EV/sales, against MGROS at 1.30× book / 0.30× sales and BIMAS at 2.50× book. If SOKM merely re-rates to MGROS's book multiple, that is ₺84 — before any earnings recovery.

SOKM can afford to lose money for several more quarters — which is exactly why it is not CRFSA.
- **The macro regime is the one this business is built for.** 29% inflation drives consumer trade-down into hard discount, and disinflation from here restores real basket value. Combined with the larger-format conversion program and high private-label penetration, the operating leverage on any margin recovery is severe: ₺3,244mn of 2026E profit on a ₺30bn market cap is a 9.3× forward multiple, and 2028E ₺7,576mn would be 4.0×.

## Bear case
- **The inflection keeps being deferred.** FY-2025 −₺2,122mn, Q1-2026 −₺736mn (annualizing *worse*), operating profit negative, ROE negative. The street's ₺21,511mn 2026E EBITDA is a 2.4× jump off the FY-25 base with one quarter of evidence pointing the other way. This is a forecast doing the work that results should be doing.
- **The cheapness has a shrinking denominator.** Book fell ₺39,022mn → ₺38,132mn in a single quarter in nominal lira — meaningfully negative in real terms. A 0.79× PD/DD against book that loses ~5%/yr of value is a materially smaller discount than it appears, which is why the valuation score carries a −10.
- **No pricing power, and the cost side is legislated.** A four-way discount price war with government price scrutiny caps the revenue line, while minimum wage — the dominant cost — is set politically and reprices every January. SOKM controls neither side of its own margin, and the ₺52.09 SMA200 has capped every rally attempt so far.

## Verdict

SOKM sits in the awkward middle of the Q/P map: **Q = 50** (a genuinely defensible business model and balance sheet attached to a P&L that does not currently work) against **P = 58** (a real ~23% discount to intrinsic value, and a technically recovering but resistance-capped tape).
