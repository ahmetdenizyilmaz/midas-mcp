## Block 2 — Sector / industry → Quality 20% · **score 68**

Turkish private hospital management.

*Supports:* structurally rising demand (ageing population, chronic-disease load); private health
insurance penetration still low and growing; **health tourism is a genuine FX earner** — MLP Care
serves patients from 200+ countries across 3 continents; bed-capacity licensing is regulated,
which functions as a **barrier to entry / moat** for incumbents with licensed beds; demand is
defensive and largely non-discretionary.

*Drags:* SGK reimbursement tariffs are administratively set and chronically lag inflation, capping
pricing power in the largest payer segment; physician and nursing wage inflation runs at or above
CPI; medical devices/consumables are import- and FX-linked on the cost side; public **şehir
hastaneleri** capacity expansion has added free-at-point-of-use competition for the SGK-funded
patient. Sector is capex-heavy, which the 37% policy rate makes expensive to fund.

Net: a good structural sector with a regulated ceiling on its best-volume segment. 68.

## Bull case

- **The quality-adjusted multiple is the whole argument.** 12.7× trailing / **10.2× 2026E** P/E and
  **4.3× 2026E EV/EBITDA** for a business earning a **32.7% FY25 FAVÖK margin** and **~16–17% real
  ROE**, against a directly comparable BIST peer (LKMNH) trading at **20× P/E on a 3.5% net margin**
  and EM/global hospital operators at ~8–11× EV/EBITDA. You are paying less for the better asset.
- **Real growth in a real-growth-scarce market.** Under TMS-29 the 1Q26 prints are already
  inflation-adjusted: **+6% revenue and +25% FAVÖK in real terms**, driven by patient/payer mix and
  efficiency — not by nominal price pass-through. Very few BIST industrials can show that in 2026.
- **Balance sheet and structure are clean.** Net debt/FAVÖK ~0.76× (2.0× on the strictest
  lease-inclusive screen definition), no bedelli, no SPK measure, dividend capacity restored
  (31 Mar 2026), 41.7% free float, ₺144 mn daily turnover. R = 1.00 with nothing to deduct — rare.

## Bear case

- **The tape is not confirming.** Price is below SMA20 and SMA50, MACD has crossed bearish, and the
  attempted push into ₺406.4 resistance is happening on **44% below-average volume**. Structure is
  intact but nobody is buying it; a 20% discount can sit unclosed for quarters, and the 15-point
  gap between Valuation (66) and Technicals (43) is exactly that tension.
- **Earnings quality and the consensus margin path.** Management credited part of the 42% 1Q26 beat
  to **monetary gains from inflation accounting and deferred tax income** — non-operating. Consensus
  correspondingly models FAVÖK margin **32.8% → 28.0%** in 2026E. If that compression lands, the
  "cheap on 2026E" case weakens materially.
- **Regulated ceiling plus flow risk.** SGK tariffs lag inflation and expanded public city-hospital
  capacity caps pricing power in the largest payer pool; separately, **49.04% foreign ownership**
  means a Turkey de-risking episode hits MPARK harder than the index, independent of fundamentals.
  The PD/DD anchor (justified ~₺375 at an 11% real COE vs ₺405 spot) is the honest reminder that
  one of three anchors says this is already fair.

## Verdict

MPARK sits in the **high-Quality / mid-Price** quadrant: Q = 71 is among the better business scores
on BIST — real growth, real ROE, clean balance sheet, zero risk deductions — while P = 57 is held
back entirely by timing (Technicals 43) rather than by value (Valuation 66, 20.6% base-case
discount, IV trend rising). That combination is what a *Buy/Accumulate* is supposed to look like:
a good business at a fair-to-cheap price during an uninteresting patch of tape, not a moment of
maximum momentum. **The horizon is 6–18 months** — this is an accumulate-on-weakness position sized
for patience, not a swing trade; note explicitly that buying at spot ₺405 gives only ~1.2:1 to T1
whereas the ₺394–400 shelf gives 2.4:1.

**What would change the stance:** an upgrade toward *Strong Buy* needs the tape to confirm —
