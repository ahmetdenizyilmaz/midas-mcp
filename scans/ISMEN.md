## Bull case
- **Best-in-class franchise demonstrably compounding real book.** A 23-25% *real* ROE through FY2024-25 (TAS 29 basis) and parent equity still +7.6% real YoY. It stayed solidly profitable at ₺846M in a quarter that pushed A1CAP to a ₺1,049M loss and GLBMD to a loss — this is what a durable franchise looks like at the bottom of its cycle.
- **The trough is macro-driven and macro-reversible.** Q1-2026 weakness is a 37% policy rate diverting savings from equities, not a broken business. The first genuine TCMB cutting sequence should re-rate both the earnings and the multiple, hard and fast — ISMEN is the highest-quality way to own that turn.
- **Structural self-hedge and unpriced optionality.** İş Portföy collects fees on the very money-market funds cannibalising equity turnover; Efes Varlık is countercyclical to credit stress; Maxis is free optionality on foreign flows returning to a market where positioning is still light. And the tape is currently defending a 6-touch floor on above-average volume.

## Bear case
- **The price already pays for the recovery.** At 1.6× book on a business earning a 10.8% run-rate ROE, normalised anchors put fair value at ~₺31 — an ~11% premium to today's ₺34.54. The comforting 6.9× trailing F/K is a trailing-earnings illusion: on the Q1-2026 run rate the same price is **15.3× earnings**.
- **The earnings contraction is severe and not yet arrested.** Operating profit −38% real and parent net profit ~−57% real YoY; parent equity down 11% sequentially despite a profitable quarter (dividend plus, most likely, FVOCI marks — a decomposition I could not verify). The next dated event, H1 results in late August, is more likely to confirm than to refute this.
- **The timing is objectively bad and the catalyst is undated.** Full bearish MA stack, price 13% below the 200-day, 31% off the 52-week high, MACD bearish, sitting 1.8% off the 52-week low. The one catalyst that matters — TCMB cutting — was explicitly *paused* in July 2026. If 37% persists into 2027, turnover stays depressed and the multiple can drift to 1.2-1.3× book (₺25-27) with nothing going wrong at all.

## Verdict

ISMEN sits in the awkward quadrant of the Q/P map: **moderate-to-good quality (Q 58) at a poor price and a poor moment (P 33)** — a genuinely excellent franchise that is simply expensive against normalised earnings and technically broken at the same time. The 42.5 score should not be read the way a 42.5 on a penny stock reads: R is a clean 1.00, there is no dilution, no tedbir, no going-concern, ~₺213M of daily liquidity and an İşbank parent. This is "right company, wrong point in the cycle," and it suits patient capital on a 12-18 month horizon far better than anything shorter.

The single variable that decides this name is the TCMB. A cut at the September or October 2026 MPC would simultaneously fix the earnings trajectory, the sector, and the chart — and would move this up a band or two quickly. Until then, the disciplined posture is to **wait rather than reach**: the ₺33.78 six-touch floor is the level that matters, and a reclaim of SMA50 (₺36.60) and the ₺37.72 shelf on volume is the trigger that converts this from a watchlist item into an accumulation. Note also that risk/reward to T1 is only 1.95:1 — below the 2:1 bar — which argues against paying up at ₺34.54 today. **Stance would upgrade on a rate cut plus a sequential earnings inflection; it would degrade toward Unattractive on a decisive weekly break of ₺33.78 on heavy volume, which would open ₺31.11 and put the ₺26-27 de-rating scenario in play.**
