## Bull case
- **A fortress balance sheet that is genuinely rare on BIST.** $5.32bn net cash and securities against $157m of borrowings, a **+$702m net FX asset position**, and clients who pre-fund the work ($478m advances, $568m billings-in-excess). ENKA cannot be forced into a bad decision by a lender, a currency or a client — in a 37%-policy-rate economy that optionality is worth real money.
- **A USD company wearing a TRY quote.** Functional currency is the dollar; construction revenue is USD or USD-indexed. Every leg of TRY depreciation translates upward into the share price without any operating effort, and the macro brief's own framing (net FX earners favoured) puts ENKAI in the best macro bucket available.
- **The two good legs are compounding while nobody watches.** Construction operating profit +11.6% YoY, rental +12.0% YoY at a 57% gross margin, costs incurred on live contracts +12.8% in a single quarter, and an 850 MW plant (~21% energy capacity) landing in Q2 2026 aimed straight at the one segment that is bleeding. A clean Q2 print in mid-August could reset the narrative quickly.

## Bear case
- **You are paying 1.24–1.36× book for a ~5–6% USD return on equity.** Roughly 61% of that book is bonds and listed equities — a buyer can replicate it at par without paying a premium. My SOTP puts base intrinsic value at **₺76 against a ₺87.60 price**, and the justified multiple at ~1.08× book. The quality is not in dispute; the price for it is.
- **The Energy segment is a structural wound, not a soft quarter.** Revenue −38.7% YoY, a **gross loss** of $(19.1)m, a $(31.3)m net loss, and merchant CCGTs caught between BOTAŞ gas costs it cannot control and power prices it cannot set. The 850 MW addition adds capacity into that same squeeze — it may deepen the problem before it fixes it.
- **Two risks the accounts make visible.** (1) A $4.4bn mark-to-market securities book routes global bond and equity swings straight through the P&L — it cost $79.7m in valuation losses last quarter alone and makes reported EPS partly a treasury result. (2) $2.36bn of investment property sits in Moscow, in RUB-functional subsidiaries, with the segment's 57% margins hostage to sanctions and repatriation. Add an 8.2% free float and minorities have no lever at all.
- Technically, the overhead is real: **96.40 rejected 5 times, 102.01 four times**, against a nearest support with a single touch and no strong floor until 74.76.

ENKAI sits in the classic **high-Q / low-P quadrant** — Quality 60 against Price 47 — which is exactly the configuration the v3.1 model is built to label a *quality watchlist* rather than a buy. This is one of the best-capitalised businesses on Borsa İstanbul, a dollar-earning contractor with $5.3bn of net cash, pre-funded working capital, clean governance on every testable dimension and no dilution risk whatsoever; the problem is not the company, it is the ~15% premium to my base intrinsic value of ₺76 and the fact that a mid-single-digit USD ROE does not earn 1.36× book. The tape agrees with the "wait" conclusion without shouting: the golden cross and the SMA200 are holding and Friday's bounce off ₺85.24 came on volume 45% above average, but MACD is still bearish, price is under both the 20- and 50-day, and **risk/reward to the first target at ₺92.20 is only 1.0:1 from here** — chasing ₺87.60 is paying for the quality twice.

**Horizon this suits:** patient, multi-year, capital-preservation money that wants USD exposure inside a TRY portfolio — not a trade. Accumulate into the ₺84.50–86.00 zone where R:R improves to 2.7:1, and size it as a core holding rather than a position.

**What would move the stance up to Buy/Accumulate:** (i) a **clean Q2/H1 2026 print in mid-August** showing the Energy segment's loss narrowing as the 850 MW plant comes online and construction revenue converting the +12.8% cost build into reported growth — that lifts Health and Catalysts together; (ii) **price back into the ₺76–82 range**, which alone would take Valuation from 38 toward the mid-50s and P into the mid-50s, printing a low-60s FINAL without a single fundamental changing; (iii) a **reclaim of SMA50 (₺92.60) on above-average volume**, which would push TechnicalTiming past 70 and trigger the **+7 tape adjustment** — worth roughly 7 points of FINAL on its own.

**What would move it down:** a second consecutive quarter of negative free cash flow, an Energy loss that widens rather than narrows after the new plant starts, a material write-down or repatriation restriction on the Moscow property book, or a decisive loss of the SMA200 at ₺88.08 with follow-through below ₺85.24 — which would open a ~₺10 air pocket down to the 2-touch support at ₺74.76 and, if the MA stack inverts, activate the freefall penalty and the −7 tape.

### Data caveats (⚠ low-confidence flag)
1. **Recent news flow is under-covered.** The session's web-search budget was exhausted before this scan began, so the last ~3 months of press and KAP headlines could not be swept. The News & Governance block was reconstructed from **primary sources instead** — the 31.03.2026 IFRS filing's own notes on dividends, related parties, litigation, share capital, treasury shares and shareholding structure, plus one dated broker note (İş Yatırım, 2026-04-24). Testable governance and dilution facts are therefore solid; a mega-contract award or KAP disclosure after 8 May 2026 could be missing.
2. **USD/TRY is derived, not quoted.** No live FX rate was available. The rate was triangulated from two hard tie-outs in the data (Q1 2026 revenue ₺35.83bn ÷ $821.823m = 43.60 average; net cash ₺183.8bn ÷ $4,141m = 44.39 at 31 Mar 2026) extrapolated along the macro brief's year-end 51.55 survey path → **≈47.5 on 2026-08-01**. Because the fair-value bridge is USD-based, the TRY fair values move ~1:1 with this assumption: at 44.4 the base FV is ₺71; at 51.55 it is ₺83. **The "modestly overvalued" conclusion holds across that entire range** — the stance is not sensitive to the FX estimate, but the precise discount% is.
3. **Q2 2026 was not yet filed** as at the scan date (H1 consolidated deadline ~19 August 2026). All fundamentals are Q1 2026, the latest available period.
4. Annualising a single quarter into run-rate earnings is a simplification; ENKA's construction revenue is lumpy and Q1 carried unusual securities marks in both directions.
