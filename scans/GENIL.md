## Bull case
- **The margin inflection is real and it is large.** Gross margin went from 21.0% (FY2025) to **29.4%** (Q1 2026) while revenue grew ~19% in real terms — that is operating leverage arriving, and it is the kind of change that re-rates a stock if Q2 confirms it on 19 August.
- **GENIL is a levered call on TCMB easing.** A 20.5% FAVÖK margin collapsing to a 5.7% net margin is an interest-expense story, not an operations story. With ₺4.39bn of mostly short-dated borrowings and the policy rate at 37% with a disinflation path intact (year-end TÜFE expectation 29.21%), each leg of easing drops almost directly to EPS. The earnings base is coiled.
- **The capex is built but not yet earning.** ₺10.29bn of fixed assets sits on the balance sheet depressing ROE today while producing little; localization preference in public procurement is the payoff. Buying at 3.6× book means paying for that payoff in advance — but the asset is real, and net debt/FAVÖK of ~0.9× means it was funded conservatively.

## Bear case
- **The price already assumes the recovery.** At ~39× TTM earnings and 3.6× book on a ~9% ROE, GENIL carries a *higher* PD/DD than SELEC (3.8) while earning half SELEC's return on equity — and SELEC does it from a net-cash position. The ROE-justified PD/DD is 1.9×, and even crediting the idle asset base fully, base fair value lands at **₺7.00 against a ₺9.32 price**. The stock is down 30% YTD and is *still* expensive; that combination is what a de-rating in progress looks like.
- **₺275m of cash against ₺4.09bn of short-term borrowings.** Leverage is low in absolute terms but the maturity profile is not, and the entire stack rolls annually at 37–40%. If easing stalls — the July MPC already paused — the interest bill that is currently eating three-quarters of the FAVÖK margin does not shrink, and the earnings inflection thesis simply does not arrive.
- **The tape has not confirmed anything.** Price is 8.65% below a falling SMA200 with no golden cross, resistance sits 1.3% overhead and a 7-touch wall at 10.04 above that, and the bounce is running on volume **19.6% below** its 20-day average.
