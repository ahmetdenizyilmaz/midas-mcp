**Bull case**
- **The cheapest asset base in the sector, and the assets are real.** 0.19× book against a 0.4–0.8× peer range, and **4.7× EV/FAVÖK against AYDEM's 7.1×** on nearly identical leverage (3.87× vs 4.11× net debt/FAVÖK). ZOREN owns diversified geothermal, hydro, wind, solar and gas capacity — with Kızıldere geothermal running as baseload renewable on USD-indexed tariffs — plus international generation and Turkey's largest EV-charging network. If the guided ₺13.5–15.0bn of FAVÖK is real, the enterprise is being valued at less than five times it.
- **The bleeding is slowing and the core turned positive.** Q1 2026's net loss narrowed to **−₺3.07bn from a restated −₺4.35bn**, and core operating profit went positive despite a 26% real revenue decline. Management published FY2026 guidance rather than going quiet. Equity has not gone negative and debt service is being met on schedule (₺372.78mn repaid on the January-2026 paper).
- **This is a geared call option on the TCMB easing cycle.** With ₺55.2bn of net debt against ₺11.65bn of market cap, the equity has ~4.7× gearing to the cost of money. The July 2026 MPC held at 37.0% with year-end inflation expected at 29.21% — a strongly positive real rate that the Council itself calls a "cautious pause". When easing resumes, no liquid name on the exchange has more torque to it, and the current price embeds essentially none of that.

**Bear case**
- **EBITDA never reaches the equity — the loss is structural, not cyclical.** FY2025: ₺39.8bn of revenue produced only **₺1,458mn of operating profit** (3.7%) and a **−₺16,184mn net loss** (−40.6% net margin). Depreciation eats ~₺11–12bn of the FAVÖK and interest on ₺55.2bn of net debt takes the rest; implied interest coverage is around **0.15×**. Reverse-engineering equity cash flow (FAVÖK ₺14.25bn − interest ~₺10bn − maintenance capex ~₺3bn ≈ **₺1.25bn**) lands fair value at ₺2.00–2.50 — i.e. *the market price is roughly right*, and the 0.19× book is not a mispricing but a solvency discount.
- **The denominator is shrinking on both sides.** Real revenue **−26% YoY** in Q1 2026, and FY2026 guidance of ₺40–43bn against 29.21% expected inflation implies a further **16–22% real decline**. Equity fell from ₺70.7bn (Sep-2025) to ₺61.1bn (Mar-2026), −13.6% in two quarters, ~20%/year at the current burn. With net debt/equity at 0.90×, a **10% write-down of the ₺135bn asset base erases 22% of the equity**. Book is converging down toward the price, not the price up toward book. That is the definition of a value trap.
- **The tape confirms the fundamentals and offers no floor.** Price is below a fully bearish MA stack (−16.7% vs SMA50, −24.5% vs SMA200), 47.4% off the 52-week high, **1.3% above the 52-week low**, below the lower Bollinger band, with MACD bearish and ROC(20) at −14.0% — the freefall pattern the backtest measured at −1.4% excess return over 63 days. The only sourced broker view (Gedik, ~May-2026) is **"Endeks ile Paralel" at ₺3.50** — neutral, and the stock has fallen a third below even that.
