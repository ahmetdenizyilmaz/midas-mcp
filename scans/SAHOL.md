## Bull case
- **The discount is real and the assets are top-tier.** At ₺84.50 the market pays ~51% of a ₺167/share sum-of-the-parts, for a portfolio whose single largest item — a 40.75% stake in Akbank — is earning a 22.9% 2026e ROE and is the most direct listed beneficiary of the TCMB easing cycle. İş Yatırım carries AL on both the parent (₺181, 24.04.2026) and the bank (₺93, 29.07.2026).
- **Structure has not broken.** Price is 8.7% below SMA50 but SMA50 still sits above SMA200; the 3-touch ₺82.75 support held on Friday with a +2.67% close on 12%-above-average volume. That is a pullback defending itself, not a distribution pattern — and the freefall penalty that punishes genuine breakdowns does not apply here.
- **Optionality that is not in the earnings line.** Enerjisa Üretim (50%), Philsa (25%), and the US solar/Climate Technologies build-out contribute little to today's depressed TMS-29 net profit but are meaningful, largely unlisted NAV — precisely the kind of value a 49% holding discount ignores.

## Bear case
- **The earnings are genuinely bad, not merely optically bad.** ROE 1.1%, ROA 0.1%, a ₺317.6mn quarterly profit on ₺63.4bn of revenue, and restated equity falling from ₺397.5bn to ₺382.4bn in a single quarter. Even allowing generously for TMS-29 monetary-loss distortion, a holding that does not grow book value in real terms is a value trap candidate, and the cheapness metric shrinks along with the denominator.
- **The consumer and construction legs are structurally, not cyclically, impaired.** CarrefourSA lost ₺1,749mn in Q1-2026 with ₺20.9bn of net debt; Teknosa lost ₺2,477mn in FY2025; Akçansa lost ₺324mn in Q1-2026 into an analyst-declared "weak sector outlook". Discounters and a 37% policy rate are not problems that one rate-cut cycle fixes.
- **The book-value cheapness is partly an accounting illusion.** The headline PD/DD of 0.46 leans on total consolidated equity swollen by the minority interest from fully consolidating a 40.75%-owned bank. On parent-attributable equity SAHOL is *not* cheaper than KCHOL (0.7) or AGHOL (0.7) — and it earns a lower ROE than either. Momentum agrees: MACD bearish, ROC20 −13.4%, price −25.3% from the 52-week high.

## Verdict
SAHOL sits in the middle of the Q/P map, not at a corner: quality is respectable-but-not-excellent (Q 57.5 — a durable franchise with near-zero current earnings power), and price is mildly attractive-but-unconfirmed (P 53.4 — a ~23% discount to a conservatively-discounted NAV, against technicals that are still pointed down). Multiplied and risk-adjusted, that is **52.4 — Hold/Neutral**, a name to own patiently or accumulate into support rather than chase, suited to a 12–24-month horizon that requires the TCMB easing cycle to actually arrive.

Two events would move it up a band toward Buy: **(1)** the H1-2026 consolidated print in mid-to-late August confirming that Akbank's ₺34.35bn half-year flows through to a visible group ROE recovery, and **(2)** a reclaim of SMA50/₺93.37 on above-average volume, which would lift TechnicalTiming above 70, trigger the +7 tape term, and — with Valuation unchanged — carry FINAL into the low 60s. Conversely, a close below ₺80.31 would put SMA50 under SMA200 within weeks, activate the freefall penalty and the −7 tape, and drop this into the Speculative band; that is the level at which the thesis is wrong, not merely early.
