## Recovery conditions *(required — FINAL < 45)*

Three concrete things must happen before this moves up a band:

1. **The rights issue must complete and clear.** Until the ₺2.5 bn → ₺3.5 bn bedelli is subscribed and the new shares are trading, there is a known, dated supply event overhead and a mechanical ex-rights reference near **₺5.26**. Dilution windows are best watched from outside.
2. **A profitability inflection, not a nominal one.** Q2/H1 2026 (due ~mid-August) needs to show ROE moving toward the high-teens/20s so the bank stops destroying real book. **+25% nominal growth against ~31% inflation is not an inflection** — it is a real-terms decline. This is the single item that would lift Health out of the 30s and raise the whole score, because Q is the exponent that compresses everything else.
3. **A genuine base on the tape, after the measures lapse.** A weekly close back above the **₺7.905** shelf (4 touches) on volume above the 20-day average, with the VBTS regime expired (21 Aug 2026) and no fresh SPK action — that would be the first evidence of a real bid rather than a dead-cat bounce off a limit-down sequence.

**Value-trap honesty:** even at ₺6.97, after a 65% collapse, SKBNK is *not* cheap on the numbers — it trades at 7.9x earnings against peers at 2.9–4.9x while earning a fraction of their ROE. Cheap-looking prices can stay cheap-looking for years when the denominator (real book value) is shrinking. A lower price alone will not fix this score; only the business will.

## Bull case

- **Capitulation is real and measurable.** Ten straight limit-down days, RSI 20.94, price at the lower Bollinger band, and a 632%-of-average volume print with ₺2.47 bn of turnover. The backtest is explicit that plain oversold on BIST does not underperform — forced sellers under an active margin-ban regime tend to be exhausted, not informed, and a sharp mean-reverting bounce toward ₺7.20-7.90 is a reasonable expectation.
- **The rights issue is a solvency positive.** ₺1 bn of fresh equity on a ₺22.5 bn base lifts capital ratios into a sector where CAR has slipped to 17%, and removes the balance-sheet tail risk that has shadowed Şekerbank for years. Once the dilution window passes, one overhang is permanently gone.
- **Maximum rate-cut leverage if the easing cycle resumes.** A liability-sensitive SME lender at 12-13% ROE has far more operating leverage to falling funding costs than a bank already earning 25%+. If TCMB cuts meaningfully into 2027, Şekerbank's earnings recovery is arithmetically the largest in the sector — and the ownership structure (a 30.71% foundation stake, a 12.06% foreign sovereign stake) means strategic-buyer interest can always resurface, even after the İlbak process was terminated.

## Bear case

- **It is the most expensive bank in the peer group on the weakest earnings.** F/K 7.90 and PD/DD 0.78 against ALBRK 3.2/0.8, AKBNK 4.9/1.0, TSKB 2.9/0.6 — while earning ~12-13% ROE versus their 21-60%. The 65% crash removed a speculative premium; it did not create a discount. Base fair value ₺4.80 sits **31% below** the current price, and even the bull-case ₺6.60 is below it.
- **The denominator is shrinking on two fronts at once.** Nominal ROE of 12-13% against ~30% inflation erodes real book by ~13% a year, and the pending 40% rights issue at ₺1.00 against ₺8.98 of book cuts BVPS by 25% to ₺6.70. Cheapness measured against a retreating book value is an illusion.
- **The regulatory and governance overhang is live, not historical.** A VBTS short-selling and margin ban is in force to 21 August 2026 (the second such episode in 2026), SPK is examining price formation in the shares against the backdrop of its 2026/44 anti-manipulation bulletin, free float was expanded to 46% via MKK conversions on 29 July 2026 — right at the top — and the company's own answer to a +170%/−65% round trip was that it has nothing to disclose. When a bank's price discovery is under regulatory examination, position sizing should assume further measures, not fewer.

## Verdict

SKBNK sits in the worst quadrant of the Q/P map: **low quality (Q 40) meeting a genuinely bad price (P 16)** — the multiplicative blend compresses hard, and a 0.64 risk overlay does the rest, landing at **15.7 → Unattractive / Reduce-into-strength**. The critical, counterintuitive point is that this is *not* a "cheap after a crash" story: at 7.9x earnings and 0.78x a book value that is being eroded in real terms and about to be cut 25% by dilution, Şekerbank is the most expensive bank in its peer group while earning the least, and the base intrinsic value of **₺4.80** sits 31% below the last price even after a 65% collapse. This suits no horizon at today's price — an oversold bounce toward ₺7.19-7.90 is plausible and is the right zone for a holder to trim into, but the 0.14:1 reward-to-risk to the first target makes it a trade for tape specialists, not an investment.

What would change the stance, concretely: the rights issue completing and clearing the ₺5.26 ex-rights reference; H1 2026 results (~mid-August) showing ROE inflecting toward the high teens rather than +25% nominal growth that is −5% real; and a weekly close above **₺7.905** on above-average volume once the VBTS regime lapses on 21 August 2026 with no fresh SPK action. Any two of those together would lift Health out of the 30s and Technicals out of the teens simultaneously — which, because Q is the compressing exponent, is worth far more to this score than any further fall in price would be. Until then, the honest framing is that this is a business problem wearing a price-chart costume, and nothing about the last two weeks has changed the business.
