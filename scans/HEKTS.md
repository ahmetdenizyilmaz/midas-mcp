## Verdict

HEKTS sits in the worst quadrant of the Q/P map: **modest quality (Q 44) at a genuinely poor price (P 28)**, with a 0.75 risk overlay for pending rights-restricted dilution and multi-quarter losses. The unusual feature of this name is that the −45% drawdown has *not* created value — three independent intrinsic methods converge on ₺1.10-2.40 against a ₺2.91 tape, so the fall has taken an extremely expensive stock down to a merely very expensive one, which is why the Valuation sub-score is 8 rather than the 70s you would normally expect near a 52-week low.

The horizon this suits is nobody's right now. For a holder, the measured path is trimming into the ₺3.20 and ₺3.39 supply shelves rather than any action at ₺2.91 — this is a reduce-into-strength rating, not a "dump it" instruction, and the OYAK recapitalisation genuinely lowers the tail risk of a permanent impairment.
