## Bull case

- **Cheapest large non-life insurer on BIST with the highest ROE in the peer set** — F/K 3.65 and PD/DD 1.18 against peers at 4.8-7.8× and 1.5-4.3×, on 38.4% average ROE (H1 2026). Simply closing half the gap to the sector's ~5.8× F/K is ₺37+.
- **Underwriting is genuinely repairing:** combined ratio −4.4pts QoQ, loss ratio −4.2pts, technical margin +200bp to 18% (Q2 2026). If the combined ratio crosses back under 100%, ANSGR stops being a bond fund with an insurance licence and becomes an insurer — that is a multiple-expansion event, not an earnings event.
- **Structurally protected franchise:** İş Bankası bancassurance distribution, ₺89bn float, no financial debt, ~5.1% dividend yield on a payout of only 21.6%, and a health book growing +42.4% into the TSS adoption wave. Technical structure is intact (golden cross, +7% over SMA200) with unusually low volatility (ATR 2.5%, annualized 20.4%) for a BIST name.

## Bear case

- **The entire profit is rented from the policy rate.** Combined ratio 112.5% means underwriting loses money; ₺89bn of float at 37% is what produces the ₺14.9bn TTM profit. The macro brief's own base case — year-end TÜFE 29.2%, 12m-ahead 23.95%, easing resuming — is the direct mechanism by which this earnings stream shrinks. Q2 already showed the fragility: **−8% QoQ** purely on the absence of a ₺700m dividend and a weak precious-metals sleeve.
- **The business is shrinking in real terms and losing share.** Premium +22% nominal vs sector +29% and ~32-33% CPI ⇒ **≈ −8% real** with share loss; motor liability **−7.4% nominal ≈ −30% real** in the most regulated line. Cheap multiples on a book that is not growing in real volume is the classic value-trap set-up.
- **Timing is neutral-to-soft.** Price sits below SMA20 and SMA50 with a bearish MACD crossover, closing exactly into a four-touch resistance on average volume — no accumulation signature, hence no tape bonus. A rejection here re-tests ₺26.44 and then ₺25.24.
