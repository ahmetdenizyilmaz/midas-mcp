### 4. Connections / value-chain (score 62)
Çimsa runs **three integrated plants domestically (Mersin, Eskişehir, Afyonkarahisar) plus a white-cement facility in Valencia, Spain**, alongside UK/Ireland and US downstream operations (company IR, 2026). It is the global leader in **white cement** — a high-margin specialty niche with genuine pricing power and low substitution risk, structurally different from the commodity grey-cement business that is dragging its domestic peers.

This is the single best argument for the name: a material share of earnings is earned in hard currency, in end-markets outside Turkey, and in a product with a defended margin. İş Yatırım's own framing (24.04.2026) matches — international weight and the high-value product portfolio "support operational resilience despite weakening domestic demand."

Held to 62 rather than higher because European construction is itself soft (recovering only as the ECB eases), and EU decarbonisation/CBAM is a real medium-term cost on the export book — partially, not fully, hedged by owning EU-based assets.

## Bull case
- **Earnings resilience is proven, not promised.** CIMSA posted +₺641 mn in Q1 2026 while NUHCM (−₺860 mn), CMENT (−₺645 mn) and BUCIM (−₺178 mn) lost money and AKCNS's F/K went unquotable. Global white-cement leadership plus Valencia/UK-Ireland/US hard-currency earnings are the structural reason, and they do not disappear with the Turkish cycle.
- **Leverage is the coiled spring.** ₺23.4bn of net debt is today's problem and tomorrow's torque. At a 37% policy rate the financing line is crushing net margin to 6.8%; each leg of TCMB easing drops straight through to earnings, which is why the 2027 net profit estimate (₺4,225 mn) exceeds FY2025 (₺3,414 mn) on essentially flat revenue.
- **Price is at a heavily-defended level with the long-term structure intact.** A nine-touch shelf at ₺43.69 sits 2.6% below, RSI is 34, and SMA50 still holds above SMA200. Valuation is undemanding on the group's best earnings (PD/DD 1.2 versus 1.4 peer median, FD/FAVÖK 7.2 versus 8.4).

## Bear case
- **The book value is shrinking in real terms.** Equity fell ₺36.56bn → ₺34.76bn in one quarter, roughly −10% real, *despite* a profit. A PD/DD of 1.2 against a denominator retreating at that pace is less cheap than it prints — this is exactly the "cheap-looking, but the denominator is shrinking" case, and it is why the valuation score carries a −10.
- **It is the only levered name in a loss-making sector.** Four of five peers hold net cash. At 2.33× net debt/EBITDA into a 37% policy rate, CIMSA has the least margin for error precisely when sector demand is weakest — and there is **zero real revenue growth** to grow out of it (+5.9% nominal over two years against ~29% inflation).
- **The tape is actively against it, and the entry math at market is poor.** Below all three moving averages, fresh bearish MACD crossover, −5% ROC20, and a −3% session on 29%-above-average volume — that is supply being distributed, not absorbed. Buying here gives 0.9:1 into T1; the trade only pays at the support tag.
