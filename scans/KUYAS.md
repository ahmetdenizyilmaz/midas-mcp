## Recovery conditions (required: FINAL < 45)

This score is **not** driven by the chart — the technical structure is the *best* thing about the name. It is driven by paying **≈22× intrinsic value** for a business with a negative operating line and ₺2.45bn of short-dated debt. Three concrete, checkable things would move it up a band:

1. **A real operating inflection, not a revaluation one.** Two consecutive quarters of **positive operating profit** (Q2-2026 and Q3-2026) on recurring rental revenue — i.e. revenue holding above ~₺150mn/quarter *in real terms* with a positive FAVÖK. Q1-2026's +₺207.5mn "profit" on a −₺75.3mn operating loss does not count.
2. **The debt wall termed out.** Refinancing or repaying a material slice of the **₺2,452mn of short-term financial debt** into long-dated maturities (an asset sale into the debt, a syndication, or a project-level facility), lifting cash meaningfully above the current ₺27.3mn. Until then a single failed roll at 37%+ rates is an existential event.
3. **The valuation gap closing from either side.** Either the price de-rating toward the ₺6-10 area (still a premium to the ₺4.45 book), or a credible, audited revaluation that multiplies NAV per share several-fold. Note that **at 16× book the price would have to fall ~90% just to reach the top of the sector's own PD/DD range** — this is a case where the arithmetic simply does not permit a Buy rating without one of these.

**Honest caveat in the other direction:** expensive can stay expensive at least as long as cheap can stay cheap. With 99.6% float, no controlling shareholder, ₺467mn of daily turnover and BIST-50 index membership, KUYAS is a flow-driven instrument, and flow has already carried it +100% off the 52-week low. The score is a statement about *value and risk-adjusted odds*, not a prediction that the next 5% is down.
