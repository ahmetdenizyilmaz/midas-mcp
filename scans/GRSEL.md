- **Minority alignment:** 22.1% free float under family control distributing **~5% of earnings** (₺150mn of ₺3,179mn), a ~0.4% yield, and a 5-year average payout of 5%. Shareholders are being asked to underwrite reinvestment — including a Delaware autonomous-vehicle venture — largely on trust.
