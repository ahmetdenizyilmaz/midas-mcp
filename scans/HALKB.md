## Bull case

- **A seven-year existential legal overhang was removed on 17 June 2026.** Indicted 15 Oct 2019 → immunity defence rejected by the Second Circuit Oct 2024 → Supreme Court denial Oct 2025 → DOJ agreement March 2026 → **case dismissed by Judge Berman, 17 June 2026**. Whatever discount the market carried for a potentially bank-breaking US penalty can now be released — a structural, dated re-rating argument that **no private peer possesses**.
- **Margin repair is already visible at the top of the P&L.** Q1 2026 net interest income of **₺38.14bn is 39.5% of the entire FY2025 NII of ₺96.52bn** — a ~₺152.6bn annualised run-rate, **+58% on FY2025**, comfortably ahead of inflation. HALKB is the most rate-geared major bank in Turkey: when TCMB resumes cutting from 37%, its deposit-heavy, policy-lending book reprices harder than anyone's. İş Yatırım carries ROE 14.1% (2025A) → 20.2% (2026E) → **23.3% (2027E)** → 26.2% (2028E), taking forward F/K to **3.8x** and forward PD/DD to **0.8x** on 2027E.
- **Sovereign backing and a thin float are real market facts.** TVF owns 91.5%; only **8.5% floats**. There is no depositor-run risk in a state bank, funding is never the binding constraint, and a thin float means very little paper for any institutional re-rating bid to buy. This is precisely why HALKB can sustain a premium P/B to better banks — and why a peer-convergence bear case can stay wrong for a long time.
- **Technically the long-term structure has not broken:** the **golden cross is intact** (SMA50 ₺42.23 > SMA200 ₺39.20), price is only −4.49% from SMA200, the 3-touch ₺36.16 shelf held, RSI is turning up from a washout at 40.3, and the MACD histogram (−0.075) is a whisker from flipping positive.

## Bear case

- **It is the most expensive bank in the sector earning the worst returns.** **F/K 8.9 and PD/DD 1.2** (İş Yatırım, 2026-08-02) against a peer set at **F/K 4.1-4.9 / PD/DD 0.7-1.1** — while generating the **lowest ROE in the group** (14.1% 2025A; ~16.8% annualised in Q1'26 vs peers at 18-29%). Investors are paying roughly **double the sector's earnings multiple for about two-thirds of the sector's return on equity.** My intrinsic build says fair value is **₺31**, ~21% below the price.
- **Capital is the thinnest among the majors — 4.96% equity/assets** (₺231.1bn on ₺4.658trn, 31.03.2026), versus GARAN 9.3% and AKBNK ~8.1%. With sector cost of risk rising (AKBNK's gross problem-loan inflows **doubled QoQ** in Q2'26) and HALKB's book concentrated in Turkey's most credit-stressed borrowers — KOBİ, esnaf, agricultural, earthquake-region — that buffer is the entire bear thesis in one number. It is also why a dilutive capital raise remains a live structural risk despite none being announced.
- **Real earnings and real book are both going backwards.** Q1 2026 net profit **₺9.51bn vs ₺12.70bn** in Q1 2025 = **−25% nominal ≈ −44% real** against ~33% trailing inflation. Equity grew 3.65% QoQ (~15.4% annualised nominal) against a **29.21%** year-end TÜFE expectation → **≈ −11% real book erosion p.a.** The "0.8x 2027E book" argument concerns a denominator that is losing purchasing power today — hence the −10 IV-honesty deduction.
- **The tape offers no support, and price is 55% of this model.** Below SMA20/50/200, MACD bearish, ROC20 **−18.07%**, **−28.69%** from the 52-week high, immediate resistance just **₺0.67** overhead — and Friday's +4.76% bounce came on **below-average volume (−1.68%)**, i.e. unconfirmed. R:R to T1 at the current print is **0.28:1**, and there is an air pocket in the support stack between ₺34.91 and ₺26.74.

## Verdict

**HALKB sits in the lower-left of the Q/P map — the least attractive quadrant this model can produce without invoking the speculative ceiling.** It is a **Q = 48** business: profitable every quarter, non-diluting, sovereign-backed, with the single largest risk in its corporate history just extinguished — but also the **lowest-ROE and thinnest-capitalised** of the Turkish majors, running negative real returns on an eroding real book. And it is a **P = 32** price: the **most expensive name in its own peer group** on both earnings and book, in a downtrend, on an unconfirmed bounce. Multiplied and taken through a 0.95 risk overlay for live US compliance obligations and a state-controlled 8.5% float, that gives **36.5 → Speculative / Weak Hold**. Note the margin: Q = 48 clears the ceiling trigger by only three points.

**This is explicitly not a "sell now" call.** A large, deeply liquid, profitable state bank with a fresh legal clean-slate, a +58% NII run-rate recovery and a modelled ROE path to 23-26% by 2027-28 is a legitimate holding for **risk-tolerant money, in small size, over a 12-24 month horizon**. What it is not, at ₺37.44, is a purchase: the risk/reward to the first resistance is **0.28:1**, and the price sits ~21% above my base intrinsic value. If held, the measured path is to trim into strength toward the ₺42.01/SMA50 barrier rather than to act on this print.

**What separates HALKB from the rest of this batch is the *direction* of the mispricing.** AKBNK and GARAN are cheap businesses at bad moments; HALKB is a **dear business at a bad moment** — the one BIST bank where the valuation block is a headwind rather than the cushion. The stance moves **up to Hold/Neutral** on either (a) a **mid-August 2026 Q2 print** showing annualised ROE through ~29% with NPLs contained, or (b) a **TCMB cut at the August or September 2026 MPC** *combined with* a daily close back above **SMA50 ₺42.23 on above-average volume** — that pairing alone lifts TechnicalTiming toward 70, fires the **+7 tape**, and carries FINAL into the high 40s. It moves **down toward Unattractive** on a loss of the **₺34.91** three-touch shelf on expanding volume (the air pocket beneath runs to ₺26.74), on a Q2 miss dragging ROE below Q1's 16.8%, or on **any announced rights issue**, which would stack a −0.10/−0.15 risk deduction on an already-compressed score.
