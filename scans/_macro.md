## Evidence (dated)

- **TCMB policy rate: 37.0%**, held at the July 2026 MPC meeting (o/n lending 40%,
  borrowing 35.5%). Easing cycle in a cautious pause; Council still targets 5%
  medium-term inflation. (TCMB, July 2026)
- **Inflation:** main trend eased slightly in June 2026; leading data implied a
  temporary July uptick. Market survey (TCMB Piyasa Katılımcıları Anketi, July 2026):
  **year-end 2026 TÜFE expectation 29.21%**, 12-month-ahead 23.95%, 24-month 17.83%.
  Disinflation path intact but slow.
- **Real rate:** strongly positive (~+8-13 pts vs expectations) — supports TRY and
  carry, compresses domestic demand and equity multiples short-term.
- **TRY:** controlled slide; survey year-end **USD/TRY expectation ≈ 51.55** (July 2026).
  Net exporters favored over FX-debt-heavy importers.
- **Equity climate:** disinflation + eventual rate cuts = medium-term tailwind for BIST;
  high real rates and TL deposit competition = short-term headwind. Foreign positioning
  still light.

## Scoring guidance derived from the above

- Rate-sensitive (banks, REITs, leveraged industrials): the 37% policy rate is the key
  drag; note it in Sector/Health, not by changing Macro.
- Exporters / FX earners (aviation, defense, autos, mining): TRY slide is a mild revenue
  tailwind.
- All TRY growth figures must be deflated by ~30-35% YoY inflation when judging "real"
  growth.
