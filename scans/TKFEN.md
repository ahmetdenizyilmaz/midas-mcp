## Bull case

- **New owner with real capacity.** OYAK's 42.8% purchase (completed 24 Jul 2026, Competition Authority-approved 30 Jun 2026) plus a board replaced within five days signals an active, not passive, owner. OYAK's own industrial capex (steel, cement, autos, energy) is a plausible captive order book for Tekfen İnşaat — the strongest structural argument for a higher normalised margin than the 3.6% FY2025 gross print.

Losses are a P&L problem, not a solvency problem, and there is no bedelli, no rights issue and no capital increase in the file. Time is affordable here in a way it is not for most turnarounds.
- **Loss trajectory is bending the right way.** Q4'25 −₺2,959 mn → Q1'26 −₺535 mn, with the YoY comparison halving (−₺959.8 mn → −₺535.0 mn). Plus real, hard-to-replicate assets: Toros Tarım as **#2 in Turkish chemical fertilizer exports**, and owned port/terminal infrastructure.

## Verdict

TKFEN sits in the **awkward lower-left of the Q/P map — mediocre business (Q 44.8), unattractive price (P 44.0)** — with the unusual twist that the price problem is *expensiveness*, not cheapness. The technical structure is legitimately one of the best in this batch (stacked rising MAs, bullish MACD, RSI 66 without overextension, no freefall and no parabolic flag → Technicals 74), and the OYAK transaction is a real, dated, governance-improving catalyst. But the ₺57bn market cap already capitalises a turnaround that has not yet appeared in a single operating line, and the risk overlay (R = 0.85) still reflects ₺6.8bn of losses across FY2025 and Q1 2026.

**This suits risk-tolerant money only, in small size, on a 12-24 month horizon — and materially better bought near ₺131-134 than at ₺154.50, where risk/reward to the first resistance is roughly 0.16:1.** The stance is *not* an instruction to sell: for an existing holder, the constructive tape and an engaged new owner justify holding with conditions, trimming into strength toward the ₺159-164 resistance band rather than exiting into weakness.

**What would change the stance — precisely:** an H1 2026 print (~mid/late August) showing operating-line improvement rather than another monetary-gain rescue, plus a dated and quantified OYAK restructuring plan, would lift Health and Catalysts enough to push FINAL into the mid-40s-to-50s (Hold). A pullback to the ₺130.59 four-touch shelf on continued fundamental repair would do more still, moving Valuation from 8 to the 40s. Conversely, **confirmation of a going-concern audit qualification, or SPK ruling out a mandatory çağrı while operations stay negative, drops this to Unattractive (~29) — verify both before committing capital.**
