## Bull case

- **The operating businesses genuinely work and pay up.** Two 100%-owned construction subsidiaries declared dividends to the parent (Dec 2025), the group bid ₺2.5bn on a public tender (29 Jan 2026), and the holding itself declared a dividend (13 Mar 2026). This is not a shell — cash moves through the structure.
- **Real growth is large and real.** Q1 2026 net profit ₺1,234.8m vs ₺504m a year earlier is +145% nominal ≈ **+84% after ~33% inflation**; revenue at ₺3,575.9m is running ~+53% real against the FY2025 average quarter. Very few BIST mid-caps are compounding at that rate in real terms.
- **A credible, funded renewable option.** 390 MWe of YEKA solar applications plus İscehisar-2 RES, with Tera Yatırım signed as investor partner in 85%-owned Astral — the pipeline has a funding answer attached, which is exactly what a company with ₺46.7m of cash needs. Add a technically clean chart (price above all MAs, golden cross, bullish MACD, RSI 54) and a non-dilutive 148% bedelsiz landing into it.

## Bear case

- **PD/DD 10.0 in a peer group that trades at 0.5–1.4.** ALARK 0.5×, AGHOL 0.7×, ENKAI 1.4× — and Turkish holdings are *supposed* to trade at NAV discounts. RALYH is priced above even VERUS (7.7×), the market's speculative comp. Base intrinsic value of ₺72 sits 70% below the ₺241.50 price; the *heroic* case of ₺110 is still 54% below it.
- **The earnings are half accounting.** Pusula Yatırım: ~50% of Q1 2026 net income was **net monetary gain** — a non-cash TMS-29 item that mechanically shrinks as inflation falls (survey: 29.21% year-end 2026 → 23.95% 12-month-ahead → 17.83% 24-month). Meanwhile the real engine stalled: gross margin 31.3% → **6.9%**, operating margin 26.4% → **2.8%**, and net profit is 12.3× operating profit.
- **Thin cash, illiquid assets, and a regulator already watching.** ₺46.7m cash against ₺3.0bn net debt and ₺14.0bn of fixed assets, in a 37% policy-rate economy, while funding a 390 MWe ambition. VBTS short-selling/margin bans were imposed **twice** (31 Mar–29 Apr and 20 Apr–18 May 2026) after a ~340% run — the tape has been disorderly before, at 87.6% annualised volatility.

## Verdict

RALYH sits in the awkward north-east-by-south corner of the Q/P map: a **middling-quality business (Q 51) at a genuinely bad price (P 40)**, where the price problem is severe rather than marginal — Valuation scores 8/100 because the shares change hands at ~3.4× a deliberately generous fair value and at 10× book against peers at 0.5–1.4×. The multiplicative blend does what it is designed to do: a constructive chart (Technicals 68, golden cross, bullish MACD) and real dated catalysts (148% bedelsiz on 23 Jul 2026, Q2 print due, YEKA pipeline) cannot rescue a 235% *negative* discount, and the R of 0.85 for repeat VBTS measures and monetary-gain-dependent earnings takes the result to **37.9 — Speculative / Weak Hold**. This suits only small-size, risk-tolerant trading money with a defined stop, and specifically not at ₺241.50 where reward-to-risk against T1 is 0.34:1; the ₺206–212 support zone is the only entry the structure justifies. The stance changes on evidence, not on price alone: a **Q2 2026 print that restores operating margin to the FY2025 level** would lift Health and the normalized-earnings fair value together and move this toward Hold; a **YEKA award converted into funded megawatts** would do the same from the asset side; a **de-rating toward ₺120 with the business intact** would get there arithmetically. Absent any of the three, this is an expensive stock with a nice chart — and the chart is the part that can change overnight.
