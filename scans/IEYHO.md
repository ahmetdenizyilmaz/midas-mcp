## Recovery conditions (required — FINAL < 45)

The gap here is valuation, not sentiment, so the conditions are unusually demanding:

1. **A profit inflection with revenue restored.** The H1 2026 report (~19 Aug 2026) showing revenue back toward the 2025 run-rate (~₺750 mn/quarter) *and* the loss narrowing materially — not a one-off gain, but operating profitability. Q1's ₺124.4 mn revenue must be shown to be a transitory or accounting event rather than the new baseline.
2. **A disclosed, dated corporate action that creates value the price can stand on** — an asset injection, a monetisation of the ₺2,400 mn of non-current assets at or above carrying value, or an acquisition that changes the earnings base. At 40x book, only a *fundamental transformation of the denominator* closes this gap; ordinary operational improvement cannot.
3. **A valuation reset.** Purely arithmetically: the price would need to fall by ~90% *or* intrinsic value would need to rise ~10x for the shares to reach even the generous ₺16.70 high case.

**Stated honestly: this is the mirror image of a value trap.** Expensive can stay expensive for a long time while momentum lasts — the tape has been overwhelmingly right for twelve months and may stay right for weeks more. But nothing in the reported fundamentals supports ₺167.50, the equity backing is eroding at ~13% per quarter, and there is no multi-touch support until ₺97.35, some 42% below. When reflexive moves of this magnitude break, they retrace violently. **The absence of a Buy rating here is a valuation judgment, not a prediction that the rally stops on Monday.**

## Bull case

- **A genuinely clean balance sheet.** Net cash of −₺6.7 mn, liabilities just 25% of assets, equity 74.5% of total assets, and ₺2,400 mn of non-current assets. At a 37% policy rate, being debt-free is a real and rare advantage — it means the company cannot be forced into a distressed capital raise imminently, and it buys management time that levered peers do not have.
- **The tape is powerful and confirmed, with nothing above it.** Bullish MA stack, golden cross, a fresh 52-week high on volume 57.5% above average, and literally zero overhead resistance. Reflexivity is real on BIST small caps: XU100 membership (~0.8% weight) brings passive and momentum flows that can sustain a move well past what fundamentals justify.
- **The 84% revenue drop may be structural rather than operational.** If Q1 2026's ₺124.4 mn reflects a deconsolidation or disposal rather than a collapse in trading, the underlying business is less impaired than the headline suggests — and the ₺2,400 mn of non-current assets could be monetised. This is the single most important unknown, and it resolves at the H1 print (~19 Aug 2026).

## Bear case

- **The valuation is the story, and it is extreme.** ₺91.05 bn market cap against ₺2.25 bn of parent equity = **40.5x book**, ₺3.02 bn of FY2025 revenue = **30x sales**, and ₺0.50 bn of annualised Q1 revenue = **183x sales** — while losing money. No listed Turkish holding trades remotely near these levels, and none of these multiples is a peer-relative judgment call; they follow directly from the company's own reported balance sheet.
- **The intrinsic value is burning, not merely stagnant.** The Q1 2026 loss of ₺290.5 mn destroyed **12.9% of parent equity in three months**, against ₺17.8 mn of cash. Losses are accelerating (FY2025 −₺782.8 mn → Q1 annualising to ≈ −₺1,162 mn), real revenue fell ~9–10% in 2025 before collapsing in Q1 2026, and headcount is down 44% YoY. Every quarter of this makes the 40x book multiple mechanically worse.
- **Momentum is showing its first crack over a structural void.** RSI 78.4 with the **MACD already crossed bearish** and the histogram negative while price makes new highs is a classic divergence — and the ruleset's own backtest documents this exact profile marking local tops on speculative small caps (CANTE −17%, TUCLK −24% to −31%). Beneath the price there is a single-touch shelf at ₺156 and then a 42% gap to the first tested support at ₺97.35.

## Verdict

IEYHO sits in the most uncomfortable corner of the Q/P map: **low quality (Q 33) meeting an extremely unattractive price (P 32)** — a shrinking, loss-making holding whose one real virtue, a debt-free balance sheet, is being consumed at ~13% of book per quarter, capitalised by the market at 40.5x that book and 183x its current revenue run-rate. The multiplicative blend is doing exactly what it was designed to do: the tape is strong and it registers (technicals 68 of 100, the largest single contributor to the Price axis), but it cannot redeem a valuation this detached, and a Q of 33 caps what price alone could ever buy. **FINAL 26.3 → Unattractive / Reduce-into-strength — for a holder, that means trimming into the strength that currently exists, scaled, not dumping into Monday's open; the trend is intact and may well extend further before it doesn't.**

The horizon this suits is essentially none on fundamentals: there is no valuation cushion at any holding period, and the first tested support is 42% below. What would change the stance — in order of power — is **(1)** the H1 2026 report (~19 Aug 2026) revealing that the revenue collapse was a disposal/deconsolidation rather than an operational failure, together with a narrowing loss; **(2)** a dated, disclosed corporate action that genuinely transforms the earnings base or monetises the ₺2,400 mn asset block at carrying value; or **(3)** a price reset toward even the generous ₺16.70 high case. Conversely, a confirmed KAP filing of a bedelli capital increase, an SPK/VBTS measure, or a going-concern qualification — none of which could be verified or ruled out in this session — would each cut R further and push the score lower still.
