## Bull case
- **Trough may be in.** Earnings fell off a cliff in H2 2025 (~₺0.41bn for the half), but Q1 2026's ₺574mn beat consensus and improved on that run-rate. Against a 0.62x book and ~4.5x FD/FAVÖK, a confirmed inflection re-rates this quickly.
- **The competitive threat is receding, and it is measurable.** Chinese brands' share collapsed from 7.8% to 5.1% in H1 2026 (−39.6% in units) — while DOAS's own share *rose* 12.4% → 12.7%, with Škoda +47% and Cupra +22%. The franchise is taking share into a shrinking market.
- **Enormous operating leverage to TCMB easing.** At a 37% policy rate, auto credit is throttled and VDF (~49%-owned) is squeezed. Rate cuts release both the volume constraint and the finance-company earnings simultaneously — plus a ~15.8% gross dividend yield is being paid while you wait.

## Bear case
- **The return profile does not justify ownership yet.** ROE 4.64% and a 1.18% net margin sit far below any sensible Turkish nominal cost of equity — the 0.62x PD/DD is a *consequence*, not an anomaly. On trough ROE the justified book multiple is 0.36x, i.e. ₺111.
- **The balance sheet is moving the wrong way, fast.** Net debt went from ₺3.1bn (end-2024) to ₺19.7bn (mid-2025) — roughly 6x in six months — with a 0.73 current ratio, while ₺6.6bn of dividends (a ~210% payout) leaves the company against ₺3.14bn of earnings. That is the parent's balance sheet being served by the subsidiary's.
- **The end market is still contracting into rising competition.** H1 2026 units −8.25% (PC −9.84%), TOASO/Stellantis is assembling a ~28%-share domestic rival, and every broker revision since FY2025 has been downward. Cheap can stay cheap.

## Verdict

DOAS sits almost exactly on the Q/P diagonal that the model calls **Hold**: a **weak-but-intact** business (Q 44.8 — depressed by a 4.6% ROE, a −76% real two-year earnings decline, and a 6x jump in net debt) trading at a **mild 7.1% discount** to a ₺205 base intrinsic value, with **decent-but-unconfirmed** timing (P 56.6). The geometric blend refuses to let the cheap book and the ₺15 upcoming dividend redeem the returns on capital, and refuses to let the weak returns cancel a franchise taking share while its Chinese competition collapses — 48.4 is the honest middle.

**This suits a patient, income-oriented, 12–24 month horizon, not a trade at ₺190.40** — chasing spot into a 10-touch resistance offers 0.38:1 risk/reward, and the 2026-08-13 ex-dividend gap is likely to deliver the ₺178–182 zone mechanically within two weeks.

**What moves it up:** Q2 results (~2026-08-18/19) confirming a sequential earnings trough with net debt stabilising; a decisive close *through* ₺190.60 on above-average volume (which alone would lift Technicals past 70 and add the +7 tape term, ~+7 to FINAL); or the first TCMB cut, which releases both auto credit and VDF at once. **What moves it down:** Q2 showing the H2-2025 collapse resuming, net debt pushing past ~2.5x FAVÖK, or a third consecutive dividend funded by borrowing — any of which would cut Health toward the low 30s and pull FINAL into the Speculative band.
