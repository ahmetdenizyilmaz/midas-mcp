## Bull case
- **A fortress balance sheet in a 37% rate regime.** Net debt/FAVÖK of **0.66x** (vs ULKER 2.25x, AEFES 1.36x) means CCOLA funds growth from cash flow while leveraged peers pay punitive TRY interest. When money is this expensive, the company that doesn't need it wins by default — and it is the single most durable advantage in the file.
- **Earnings have inflected, and the confirming print is days away.** Q1 2026 net profit of ₺5,237 mn against an implied ₺1,709 mn a year earlier (+206% nominal, ~+135% real) reversed a poor 2025. The H1 report lands on the seasonal peak quarter with 22.8% TTM ROE and 18–20% FAVÖK margins behind it.
- **A premium franchise at a non-premium multiple, in an uptrend with room.** 7.1x FD/FAVÖK for an exclusive Coca-Cola bottler across 12 countries and 600 mn people — with per-capita consumption in Pakistan, Bangladesh and Central Asia far below global averages — is undemanding versus global bottlers at 6–9x. Technically: full bullish MA stack, golden cross, RSI at 59 with headroom, coiling below the 52-week high.

## Bear case
- **The price has already done the work; the R:R has not.** +105% off the 52-week low and 2.6% below the 52-week high, with the nearest support (88.25) carrying a **single** touch and a −13% air pocket beneath it to the first 3-touch level at 76.12. R:R to T1 is **1.44:1 at the 88–89 entry and 0.41:1 at today's ₺91.05** — the setup is good, the current price is not.
- **The valuation cushion is thin and the catalyst cuts both ways.** A 7.1% discount to a ₺98 base case is inside the noise band of the estimate itself, and PD/DD of 3.1 against peers at 0.5–1.1 leaves nothing priced for disappointment. An H1 miss on Turkish volumes has an obvious −13% technical destination.
- **The domestic demand and tax squeeze is structural, not transitory.** A 37% policy rate against ~29% inflation compresses exactly the real disposable income that sparkling volumes depend on, while sugary-drink ÖTV remains a live budget lever and a 22.8% *nominal* ROE is only around break-even in real terms. With 85% foreign ownership of a 29.3% float, a flow reversal would hit price well before fundamentals changed.

## Verdict
CCOLA sits in the model's most comfortable quadrant — **good business (Q 69), reasonable price (P 65)** — and is the rare BIST name where quality, valuation and tape currently agree rather than trade off against one another. The 73.8 is a legitimate Buy/Accumulate, but it lands at the *top* of the band on a **+7 tape term earned by a 2.15% volume margin**, so the rating is more fragile than the headline number suggests: without the tape it is a 66.8, still Buy but unremarkable.
