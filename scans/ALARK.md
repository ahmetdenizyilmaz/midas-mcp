## Bull case
- **The structure finally matches the assets.** From H2 2026 Alarko consolidates 100% of MEDAŞ/MEPAŞ/MESAŞ. A regulated, inflation-plus-real-return annuity moves from a footnote into the income statement — the single most plausible mechanism for compressing a ~46% NAV discount toward the ~38% peer norm, worth roughly ₺117–140 per share on my own multiples.
- **The balance sheet is not the problem.** Net debt ₺12.4bn against ₺82.3bn equity (leverage 0.36), and a ₺1.33bn cash dividend paid in May 2026 through a loss year. Downside here is valuation risk, not solvency risk — and at 0.53x book versus a 0.62x peer average, a lot of pessimism is already in the price.
- **Earnings have inflected.** Q1 2026 net profit ₺153m against a ₺2.87bn loss a year earlier, with the coal-price-deck exposure ($67 → ~$60/MWh guided) handed to Cengiz. The sell side agrees on direction: İş Yatırım ₺185 (Feb-2026), consensus ≈₺151, no Sell ratings.

## Bear case
- **Profitability is still theoretical.** ROE ~0%, holding-level FAVÖK negative in both FY2025 (−₺1.17bn) and Q1 2026 (−₺353m), current ratio 0.89. Consolidation changes the *presentation* of earnings; it does not by itself create them, and the market may wait for two or three clean quarters before paying up.
- **Real value is quietly leaking.** Revenue −33% real in FY2025 and −4% real in Q1 2026; equity rose 0.6% nominal in a quarter with ~6–7% inflation. At 30%+ TÜFE, a business standing still in nominal terms is shrinking — hence the −10 IV-honesty deduction, and hence "cheap" here can stay cheap.
- **The EPDK 5th-period reset is a coin-flip on the core asset.** The tariff period that sets the regulated asset base and real return for the next five years is being reset right as distribution becomes ~all of the earnings story. An unfavourable parameter set would hit the exact asset the bull case rests on. Add a 37% policy rate taxing a capex-heavy utility and a tape with no buyers (volume −20% vs average).

## Verdict
ALARK sits almost exactly in the middle of the Q/P map — a solvent, asset-rich, low-return holding (Q 53) at a modestly cheap price in an uninspiring moment (P 54), which is the textbook definition of Hold. The re-rating thesis is real and dated rather than hopeful: full consolidation of MEDAŞ/MEPAŞ from H2 2026 is the mechanism that could take the NAV discount from ~46% toward the ~38% peer norm, but it is a *presentation* fix until two or three quarters of positive consolidated FAVÖK prove the earnings are there. This suits patient capital on a 12–18 month horizon, accumulated near the ₺100.18 support rather than chased — and note the R:R to T1 is only ~1.1:1, so only the ₺111.60 target leg pays for the risk.

**What moves it up:** a Q2/Q3 2026 print showing consolidated distribution revenue with positive group FAVÖK (Health 45 → 55+ takes Q to ~58 and FINAL into the low 60s = Buy/Accumulate), or a reclaim of ₺105.19 on above-average volume (Technicals ≥ 70 unlocks the +7 tape term, worth ~7 points outright).
**What moves it down:** an adverse EPDK 5th-period parameter set, a Q2 relapse into net loss (R deduction widens to −0.10 and Health falls), or a loss of the ₺96.06 six-touch support, which would put SMA50 below SMA200 and arm the freefall penalty plus the −7 tape.
