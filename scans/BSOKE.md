**Score: 55** — the value-chain position improves in scale, but the group's growth capital and the land optionality are being routed away from this ticker.

A low score is not a timing signal, and this scan is not an instruction to sell.

Net debt/equity is 22%; there is no going-concern qualification and no VBTS measure. The company has time.

FD/FAVÖK ≈ 168× TTM vs a sector average of 14.15×. EV/Sales 10.4× against a 2-4× norm.
