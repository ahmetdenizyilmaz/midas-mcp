## Bull case

- **The protection thesis is real and dated.** Turkey's flat-steel safeguard measures underpin a forecast near-doubling of FAVÖK from ₺20.45bn (FY2025) to ₺37.17bn (2026E) and a ~19x jump in net profit to ₺10.69bn. On that number, FD/FAVÖK falls from 13.6x to **8.9x** and net debt/FAVÖK from 1.54x to **0.85x** — a genuine and rapid de-rating in the company's favour if delivered.
- **The balance sheet is not the risk.** ₺31.5bn net debt against ₺295.5bn equity (~11% gearing) means Erdemir can wait out the cycle indefinitely. Moody's B2 with a **positive** outlook (July 2026) says the credit market agrees. Cyclicals that survive their trough with clean balance sheets are the ones that capture the recovery.
- **Dominant, integrated, structurally advantaged franchise** — Turkey's principal flat-steel producer with captive ore and coke, ~12,833 employees, OYAK ownership, and ~₺4bn of daily turnover meaning any position size is executable. The trend confirms the thesis is being priced: full bullish MA stack, golden cross, +57.9% over twelve months.

## Bear case

- **The company currently earns essentially nothing.** FY2025 ROE of **0.18%** and a 0.24% net margin, with Q1 2026 at 0.64%, against ~29% inflation and a 37% policy rate. Real returns are deeply negative and inflation-adjusted equity fell **₺21bn (−6.7%) in a single quarter**. The intrinsic value is shrinking while the share price rises — the two are moving in opposite directions.
- **The price has outrun every professional estimate.** Consensus target ₺38.94 and İş Yatırım's ₺41.45 (24.04.2026) both sit *below* spot ₺42.54. All three of our intrinsic anchors — 7.5x normalized EBITDA (₺38.4), justified P/B (₺21), normalized EPS × justified F/K (₺21-24) — land at or below the price. Meanwhile KRDMD trades at 0.5x book and 6.3x EV/EBITDA versus EREGL's 1.0x and 13.6x. The entire recovery is already in the price, and it has not yet appeared in the accounts.
- **The core plant is the problem, and it is a company problem, not a market one.** Subsidiary ISDMR alone earned ₺6.7bn in FY2025 and ₺1.5bn in Q1 2026 while the consolidated group earned ₺563mn and ₺384mn — the parent's own flat-steel operations are a multi-billion-lira drag.
