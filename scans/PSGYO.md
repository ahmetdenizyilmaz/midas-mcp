## Verdict

PSGYO sits in the awkward quadrant of the Q/P map: **low quality (Q 41, below the 45 speculative line) at a price that is not actually cheap (P 48; ~3% above a ₺3.39 base fair value, and a narrower discount to book than its own peer group)**. The constructive MA stack is real but unconfirmed — no freefall, no parabolic extension, but volume at 27% of average earns no tape bonus — so there is no flow argument to override the fundamentals. The result, 38/100, is an honest **Speculative / Weak Hold**: this suits only risk-tolerant money, in small size, on a multi-year horizon, and it is explicitly **not** an instruction to sell.

The bull case is not empty — a ₺14.95bn signed Emlak Konut contract against a ₺24.1bn market cap is a serious pipeline, insiders funded it with ₺5bn of their own cash, and this is the highest-beta balance sheet in the sector to the TCMB easing that the macro brief expects. But that case is years out and rate-dependent, while the melting is happening now: a 6% nominal ROE against 29% inflation erodes real NAV every quarter the discount fails to close.

**What would change the stance:** a Q3-2026 print (~late Oct-2026) showing profit sourced from deliveries rather than revaluation, plus real NAV per share holding its purchasing power, would lift Health and remove the −10 IV-honesty charge — worth roughly a full band.
