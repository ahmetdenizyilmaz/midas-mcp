## Block 4 — Connections / value-chain → **62**

İşbank is not a pure bank; it is the hub of Turkey's largest private industrial-financial group. The participation portfolio gives it exposures a standalone bank lacks: **Şişecam** (glass, heavily export-weighted — a direct beneficiary of the controlled TRY slide to ~51.55 year-end), **Anadolu Hayat Emeklilik** (private pension/BES, a structurally compounding AUM pool as Turkish auto-enrolment matures), **TSKB** (development/sustainable finance), **İş Yatırım** (capital markets, which earns well in high-turnover BIST regimes), plus REIT, leasing, factoring and technology arms.

End-market is the whole Turkish economy — cyclical and currently soft under +8-13pt real rates, which is what holds this at 62 rather than higher. But diversification here is genuine, the substitutes risk is low, and two of the verticals (pensions, export glass) are structural growers rather than rate-cycle plays. **62.**
