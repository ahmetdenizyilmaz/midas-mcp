- **Substitutes:** commercial banks moving into green lending; large corporates accessing DFIs directly.

**Judgment: 62** — a better-than-average value-chain position whose realisation is deferred by the rate cycle.

## Bull case

- **The cheapest bank in the peer group earns the highest ROE in it.** P/E 2.9 vs peer 4.55 and P/B 0.61 vs peer 0.95, on ROE of 24-29% against a peer average of 22.1% — a genuine cross-sectional anomaly, not an artefact of a broken denominator.
- **The funding moat is real and shows up in the returns.** Long-tenor DFI lines produce ROA of 3.2% — roughly 1.5× what deposit-funded Turkish banks manage — with CAR at 19%, no dilution risk, and no exposure to the TL deposit-cost war.
- **A structural, financeable demand pool is queued behind the rate cycle.** EU CBAM and the energy transition force Turkish exporters into decarbonisation capex, and TSKB is the primary domestic channel for DFI and climate-fund money. İş Yatırım's ₺20.00 target (24.04.2026, +83%) is what the re-rating case looks like if it lands.

## Bear case

- **Everything is contracting in real terms.** Derived H1'26 net profit −9.5% nominal YoY = ~−32% real; net interest income run-rate flat nominal = deeply negative real; book value eroding ~13-15% real per year. ROE has glided 29.1% → 24% and now sits *below* the 29.21% year-end inflation expectation — a nominally excellent bank earning a roughly zero real return on equity.
- **TSKB is on the wrong side of the coming easing trade.** With no deposit base, it captures far less funding-cost relief than AKBNK/YKBNK/GARAN when TCMB cuts — so the sector's most likely re-rating catalyst is precisely the one that helps this name least. That is a defensible reason for the discount to persist rather than close.
- **The tape is confirmed against it.** Below all three MAs in bearish order, MACD bearish and still widening, ROC20 −9.44%, 3.4% off the 52-week low on flat volume — no accumulation footprint anywhere. Four resistance shelves within 11% overhead make R:R to the nearest target worse than 1:1.

## Verdict

On the Q/P map TSKB sits **upper-middle on Quality (62) and low on Price (43)** — and it is worth being precise about *why*, because the label understates the franchise. This is not a speculative business: it is a well-capitalised, high-ROE, cleanly-audited İş Bankası subsidiary with ₺158 mn of daily turnover and a zero-deduction risk overlay (R = 1.00). The score is low because the *setup* is poor, not because the company is: the Valuation sub-score is decent (56 after the honesty haircut) but the confirmed freefall pattern drags Technicals to 28 and levies a −7 tape, and under the price-weighted 0.55 exponent that is what pins the blend at 43.7. Read this as a **quality name in a confirmed downtrend — a watchlist entry, not a purchase**, and emphatically not an instruction to sell.

The horizon that suits it is **long — 12 months plus, and only for money that can sit through a real-terms grind** — because the value gap is genuine (~22% to a ₺14.00 base) but has no dated mechanism to close it, and the book is shrinking ~13-15% a year in real terms while you wait.

Failing both, the ₺10.65 two-touch support is the line that matters; losing it opens 10.11 and 9.70.
