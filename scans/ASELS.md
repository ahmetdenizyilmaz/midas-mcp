## Bull case

- **Backlog is the moat: US$20.7 bn, an all-time record and the first crossing of $20 bn** — roughly 4-5 years of revenue already contracted. Almost nothing else on BIST offers that visibility, and it makes the earnings stream unusually forecastable in a country where forecasting is otherwise futile.
- **The growth is real, not inflationary.** Under TMS-29, Q1 2026's +15% revenue / +28% FAVÖK / **+86% net income** are purchasing-power-adjusted. A ₺1.75 trn national defense budget (+34.6%) and the European rearmament cycle are funded, multi-year, and already converting into signed contracts (€780 mn in June alone).
- **Pristine financial risk profile:** 30.9% FAVÖK margin, **0.35x net debt/FAVÖK**, no dilution, no regulatory measures, deep liquidity, 51.5% foreign ownership. R = 1.00 with nothing to deduct — a genuinely rare scorecard on this exchange.

## Bear case

- **The price already contains the story.** 44x trailing / ~35x forward earnings and **5.55x book** against a real ROE of only ~12.6%. On a justified-PD/DD basis the stock is worth a fraction of where it trades; the market is capitalizing an ROE roughly double what the company currently earns. That is a *long* way to fall if the rearmament premium deflates.
- **Momentum has already rolled over.** −23.9% from the 52w high, below SMA20 and SMA50, MACD bearish, ROC(20) −11%. The stock is still **+105% off its 52w low** — the crowded trade is unwinding, and 316 → 303 → 285 are the next places it can rest.
- **Structural blemishes the multiple ignores:** single dominant state customer with negotiated pricing, unverified cash conversion (Turkish primes are chronically receivable-heavy — I could not source dated FCF), imported-component and export-licence dependency, and a 37% policy rate that makes a 1.6%-yielding 44x-earnings equity a hard sell against TL deposits.

## Verdict

ASELS sits in the top-right-then-left quadrant of the Q/P map: **the business is excellent (Q 79) and the moment is not (P 46)** — record backlog, real double-digit growth, an unlevered balance sheet and an empty risk overlay, all trading ~12% above my ₺305 base fair value while the tape corrects. The geometric blend does exactly what it is designed to do here: quality earns the stock a 58.7 rather than the low-40s a weaker business would print on the same chart, but it cannot manufacture a Buy out of a full price and a bearish MACD. This is a **hold-if-owned, wait-if-not** for a multi-year horizon — the structural defense cycle is real and I want to own it, just not at ₺342 with 0.42:1 reward-to-risk.

Two things change the stance and both are concrete: a **hold of ₺316 support on rising volume**, or a **reclaim of the ₺374.6 SMA50 on above-average volume** (which would arm the +7 tape term and carry FINAL to ~73). A close below ₺303.75/SMA200 on volume is the line where I stop calling this a pullback.
