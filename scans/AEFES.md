## What would move this up a band (to Buy, 60+)

Not required at 58.2, but the triggers are narrow enough to name:
1. **A clean 2Ç2026 beat on 11-Aug-2026** with Türkiye beer volume returning to growth on the EFES relaunch, and Beer Group net debt/FAVÖK moving back below ~4.0x. This lifts Health and Catalysts together and is the single highest-leverage event.
2. **Any resolution of the Russian asset** — partial compensation, a negotiated exit, or conversely a decisive write-down that removes the ₺56.6bn overhang. Either outcome improves the honesty of the book; the current limbo is what costs it 10 valuation points and 0.05 of R.
3. **A pullback into ₺19.50-20.00 that holds on volume.** Nothing about the business needs to change — at ₺19.75 the base-case discount widens to ~14% and Valuation alone adds ~4 points to P, which with the tape intact prints a Buy.

## Bull case

- **You are buying the CCI stake and getting the brewer thrown in.** 50.3% of Coca-Cola İçecek is worth ₺128.15bn at CCOLA's ₺91.05 — against AEFES's entire ₺129.55bn market cap. The implied Beer Group EV of ~₺39bn on ~₺7.95bn LTM FAVÖK is **4.9x**, versus Heineken at 7.8x and a global alcohol median of 8.8x (Q2 2026). Closing even half that gap is worth ~₺2-3/share.
- **The operating turn is real and already in the numbers, not a forecast.** 1Ç2026 consolidated FAVÖK **+57.6% in real terms** with margin up 433bp to 13.6%; gross margin +410bp; CCI's margin up 491bp to 17.8% with net profit +213.8% real; group net debt/FAVÖK down from 2.0x to 1.3x; the FCF drag two-thirds smaller. Q1 is the seasonal trough — Q2 is when beer earns.
- **The tape agrees, and it is not stretched.** Full bullish MA stack with a golden cross, bullish MACD crossover, RSI only 57.9, volume 11.6% above its 20-day average, annualized vol 34.6% — low for BIST. This is a grind higher on participation, not a small-cap blowoff, and the parabolic-extension test the backtest calibrated for is nowhere near tripping.

## Bear case

- **You are also buying a ₺56.6bn phantom.** Forty-three percent of parent book sits in a Russian brewing business seized by presidential decree on 30-Dec-2024, renamed, and run by a Russian trustee — no control, no dividend, no exit. It is carried at full value. Strip it and PD/DD goes from a comfortable-looking **1.09x to 2.07x**. The FY2025 accounts even booked a ₺3.67bn *non-cash gain* from the associated FX-reserve reclassification, which flattered a year in which underlying profit fell 34.5%.
- **The half of the company that bears the name is loss-making and levered.** Beer Group: volume −9.6%, real revenue −8.4%, FAVÖK **−₺761.0mn**, net loss −₺327.3mn, and net debt/FAVÖK **4.6x and rising** — with Türkiye Beer alone carrying ₺42.0bn of net debt at a 37% policy rate. Türkiye beer faces annual excise escalation, a total ad ban and a 22:00-06:00 retail ban; its real revenue fell 18.6%.
- **Returns do not clear the hurdle, and half of what there is belongs to someone else.** ROE ~7.3% (≈11.5% ex-TMS-29) against ~29% expected inflation and a 37% policy rate. Fifty-six percent of 1Ç2026 group profit went to minorities. 4Ç2025 was a −₺3.14bn loss. And the SOTP gap that makes the bull case has already largely closed — CCOLA is within 3% of its own 52-week high, AEFES within 5.2% of its.

## Verdict

AEFES sits almost exactly in the middle of the Q/P map — a **Q of 53** (a decent, improving, but sub-inflation-return business with a stranded Russian asset and a levered loss-making beer core) paired with a **P of 60** carried almost entirely by the technical axis rather than by cheapness: at ₺21.88 the stock is within 5% of my ₺23.00 SOTP base, which is *fair value*, not a discount. The +7 tape adjustment is fully earned — the bullish MA stack, fresh MACD crossover, RSI at 58 and above-average volume are exactly the confirmed-constructive pattern the backtest validated — and it is what lifts a ₺51 raw score to **58.2, the top of Hold**, two points short of Buy.

This suits a **6-to-12-month horizon for someone who wants Central Asian beverage growth at a holding-company discount and can tolerate the Russian overhang** — but it does not suit buying at ₺21.88, where the reward to the first resistance is a third of the risk to a sensible stop. Own it into weakness at ₺20.80-21.00, add at ₺19.55, stop ₺20.00 on the first tranche.

Three things change the stance. A **2Ç2026 beat on 11-Aug-2026** with Türkiye beer volumes inflecting and beer leverage back below 4x moves it to **Buy**. **Any resolution of the ₺56.6bn Russian carrying value** — compensation, exit, or an honest write-down — removes the −10 valuation haircut and half the risk deduction. Conversely, a Q2 miss that confirms Türkiye beer volume decline is structural rather than weather-and-Ramadan, or a break of ₺19.565 on rising volume, would take Health and Technicals down together and put this into **Speculative** territory quickly — the geometric blend punishes both axes falling at once.
