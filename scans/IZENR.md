- **Spark‑spread compression is the sector story, and it is visible in this company's own P&L:** gross margin 12.9% (Q1 2025) → **1.2% (Q1 2026)**. - **Structural transition risk:** coal is the merit‑order loser. Rising solar/wind capacity pushes thermal plants down the dispatch stack and cuts capacity factors; Turkish carbon‑pricing/ETS groundwork adds a long‑dated cost overhang.
- **Offsets:** baseload thermal retains grid‑firmness value and capacity‑mechanism payments; the plant is modern (commissioned 2014).

Sector = **40** — a regulated, price‑capped, FX‑input, transition‑challenged corner of the utilities space.

**Bull case**
- **A fortress balance sheet in a 37%‑rate economy.** Net debt ₺325mn against ₺18.1bn equity (0.16× FAVÖK) and a 7.6× current ratio. This company cannot be killed by its lenders — it has the staying power to wait out a bad spark‑spread cycle, which most leveraged Turkish IPPs do not.
- **The earnings power is real when the spread opens.** FY2025 delivered ₺2.31bn FAVÖK at a 23.1% margin and ₺765mn of net profit. Q1 2026's 1.2% gross margin is a cyclical trough, not a broken asset — cheaper coal or a higher power‑price ceiling flows straight to the bottom line with enormous operating leverage.
- **Management is buying its own stock** (buyback disclosed 2026‑07‑29), the float is tight at ~26%, and daily turnover of ~₺1.6bn means a sentiment reversal can move this name fast.

**Bear case**
- **You are paying ~1.95× intrinsic value for a 3.5% real ROE.** F/K 33.7×, FD/FAVÖK 11.0×, PD/DD 1.16× — and the PD/DD only looks benign because the book is revalued PP&E that isn't earning its cost of capital. The market is paying full replacement cost for a plant that currently destroys purchasing power.
- **The Q1 2026 deterioration is severe and broad:** revenue −25% real, gross profit −93%, FAVÖK −50%, net loss widened to −₺565mn, and cash fell ₺1,483mn → ₺321mn in one quarter. The comfortable cash cushion is largely spent, and the Q2 print due by ~19 August is more likely to confirm than refute this.
- **Structural, not just cyclical:** USD fuel against ceiling‑capped TRY revenue, a single related‑party customer in a weak steel market, and a coal asset facing merit‑order displacement and carbon‑cost overhang. Technically the stock is below all moving averages with a bearish MACD and just distributed on 2× volume — and if SMA50 crosses under SMA200 the freefall flag activates and takes another 7 points off this score.

**Verdict:** IZENR sits in the worst quadrant of the Q/P map — a low‑quality earnings profile (Q 39, dragged by a 3.5% real ROE and a 93% collapse in gross profit) meeting a genuinely expensive price (P 21, roughly double my ₺4.40 base fair value). The unlevered balance sheet is a real asset and is why this rates 23.9 rather than something far worse, but a fortress balance sheet is a reason the company survives, not a reason the *stock* is worth ₺8.60. For a holder the measured path is trimming into the ₺9.54 / ₺9.96 / ₺10.39 resistance shelves rather than selling into the ₺8.53 support — this is not an urgent exit, and the July buyback plus 2× turnover means bounces are likely. The stance changes on evidence, not hope: a Q2/Q3 2026 print showing gross margin back above ~10% with FAVÖK over ₺450mn and positive operating cash flow, or a de‑rating into the ₺4.50–5.50 zone, would move this up a band or two.
