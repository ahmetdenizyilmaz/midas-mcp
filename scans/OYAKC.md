## Valuation method — intrinsic anchors only

*No support/resistance level was used in fair value (they are price-derived; that would be
circular). They appear only in Levels.*

Inputs: shares 4,861.7mn · book value/share **₺14.27** (Q1 2026 equity ₺69,393.9mn, already
TMS-29 inflation-restated) · net cash **+₺8.2bn** · TTM revenue ≈ ₺59.3bn ·
TTM net profit ₺8,435mn (EPS ₺1.735) · TTM FAVÖK ≈ ₺15.1bn.

**A. Normalized EPS × justified peer F/K (weight 25%).**
Through-cycle net margin ~11.5% (below FY2024's 16.1% and FY2025's 15.5%, which were
flattered by a stronger cycle and monetary gains; far above the current 1.0%) on ₺59.3bn
revenue → normalized net profit **₺6.8bn**, normalized **EPS ₺1.40**. Justified F/K **9.0x**
(sector trades 7.7–11.7x on *depressed trailing* earnings; a 37% policy rate compresses
multiples, a net-cash quality premium partly offsets). → **FV ≈ ₺12.6**

**B. Justified PD/DD from normalized real ROE (weight 25%).**
Normalized real ROE = 6.8/69.4 = **9.8%**. Book and earnings are both TMS-29 real, so the
cost of equity must be real too: normalized real CoE ~11% (real risk-free settling ~6% post-
disinflation + ~5% ERP), real g 2%. Justified PD/DD = (9.8−2)/(11−2) = **0.87x** →
0.87 × ₺14.27 = **FV ≈ ₺12.4**. (At today's extraordinary ~15% real CoE it would be ₺8.6 —
used as the stress case, not the base.) Peer PD/DD context: CIMSA 1.2 · NUHCM 1.4 ·
AKCNS 1.7 — OYAKC's 1.42 is mid-pack, not a discount.

**C. FD/FAVÖK on normalized FAVÖK less net debt (weight 50% — most reliable for cement).**
Normalized FAVÖK margin ~24-26% (FY2025 26.1%, trough Q1 2026 21.4%) on ₺59.3bn →
**₺14.2–15.5bn**. Justified FD/FAVÖK **6.0–7.0x** (CIMSA 7.2x levered, AKCNS 11.7x on a
loss-making base; EM cement with CBAM capex ahead). Mid case 6.5x on ₺15.5bn:
EV ₺100.8bn **+ net cash ₺8.2bn** = ₺109.0bn ÷ 4,861.7mn = **FV ≈ ₺22.4** (range ₺19.2–22.4).
*This is the anchor that most supports the bulls — the 5.9x trailing FD/FAVÖK is the one
metric on which OYAKC is genuinely the cheapest of its peer group.*

**D. Analyst cross-check (not weighted):** İş Yatırım AL, TP ₺38.92 (24 Apr 2026). Materially
above every intrinsic anchor; it caps the high case rather than setting the base.

**Blend → FV base = 0.50(21.0) + 0.25(12.6) + 0.25(12.4) = ₺16.8**
- **Low ₺13.5** — trough margins persist, FAVÖK ₺13bn at 5.5x, real ROE 8%.
- **Base ₺16.8**
- **High ₺25.0** — cycle recovers to FY2025 FAVÖK ₺16.2bn at 7.0x plus net cash, with a
  multiple re-rating as TCMB cuts.

**Discount% = (16.8 − 20.30)/16.8 = −20.8%** → a **~21% premium** to base intrinsic value.
Score 34 (the ruleset's "clearly expensive" zone is 10-25; a 21% premium is moderately, not
egregiously, expensive — and the FD/FAVÖK anchor keeps it out of the teens).

**IV-honesty adjustment: −10 (declining, not burning).** Real revenue −41% YoY and real book
value down ~7% in the quarter — intrinsic value is genuinely receding. It is **not** "burning":
the company remains profitable every quarter, equity is not being consumed by losses, and no
dilution funds operations. → **Valuation = 34 − 10 = 24**.

## Bull case
- **Fortress balance sheet in the worst possible rate regime.** ~₺8.2bn net cash and negative
  net debt/FAVÖK while CIMSA carries ₺23.4bn of net debt at a 37% policy rate. OYAKC earns
  carry through the trough, can fund CBAM/decarbonisation capex without dilution, and is the
  natural consolidator if a levered peer is forced to sell assets.
- **The cheapest cash-flow multiple in the peer group, and the trough may already be behind.**
  FD/FAVÖK 5.9x vs CIMSA 7.2x and AKCNS 11.7x — and NUHCM's H1 2026 print implies a ~+₺1.0bn
  swing in Q2 alone. If OYAKC delivers a comparable Q2 by the ~19 Aug 2026 deadline, the
  entire "1.0% net margin" narrative resets in one report.
- **Irreplaceable franchise, top-tier owner.** #1 domestic capacity, 7 plants, 7 regions,
  backed by Taiwan Cement (~112mt global, CIMPOR) — with a real dividend record (₺1.00/share,
  30 Oct 2025) and not one dilution in a 39-year listing history.

## Bear case
- **Earnings have effectively vanished and the price hasn't adjusted.** Q1 2026 net profit
  ₺102.5mn vs ₺1,303.8mn a year earlier (−92% nominal); revenue −41% *real*; net margin
  15.5% → 1.0%. Yet the stock still carries F/K 11.7 and PD/DD 1.42 — a full multiple paid
  for a trough. On the Q1 run-rate, forward F/K is ~240x. Every one of my intrinsic anchors
  lands **below** ₺20.30.
- **The demand recession is macro-driven and not in OYAKC's control.** A 37% policy rate with
  a +8-13pt real rate is the direct cause; the July 2026 MPC *held*. Cement demand does not
  recover before mortgages do, ~120mt of national capacity keeps pricing power weak, and CBAM
  raises export costs from 2026.
- **Timing is against it and the tape is deteriorating.** Below all three MAs in a fully
  bearish stack, −28.9% from the 52-week high, −26.3% vs BIST-100 YTD, and Friday's −2.96%
  arrived on 30%-above-average volume — distribution into the ₺20.19 support. Break ₺20.19,
  lose ₺19.54, and this becomes a confirmed freefall (tape → −7) heading for the ₺18.615
  52-week low. Entering at today's price gives 0.83:1 R:R.

## Verdict
OYAKC sits in the awkward north-west of the Q/P map: **Quality 49 is respectable — a net-cash,
market-leading franchise with a top-3 global parent — but Price 37 is the problem**, because
after a −41% real revenue year the shares still trade at a ~21% premium to my ₺16.8 base
intrinsic value and at a full 11.7x trailing P/E on earnings that fell 92%. The multiplicative
blend does exactly what v3.1 intends: good business, wrong price, wrong moment, and no tape
confirmation in either direction → **39.9, Speculative / Weak Hold**. This is *not* a sell
instruction; it is a "the cheapness isn't there yet" verdict. **The horizon that suits it is
long (12-24 months, a full rate cycle), the size is small, and the R:R only becomes acceptable
nearer ₺19.5.** Three things would change the stance: an H1 2026 report by ~19 Aug 2026 showing
the same Q2 inflection NUHCM already delivered (moves Health, Catalysts and Valuation together
— the single highest-leverage event, three weeks away); a resumption of TCMB cuts reviving
mortgage-led cement demand; and a technical reclaim of ₺21.34 on above-average volume, which
would lift Technicals toward 70 and unlock the +7 tape term — that combination alone would
carry FINAL into the low-to-mid 50s (Hold), and a genuine earnings recovery would take it to
Buy. Conversely, a break of ₺19.54 confirms the freefall pattern, applies −7, and drops this
into the high-20s.
