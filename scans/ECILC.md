- **Defensive end-markets with an ageing tailwind.** Pharma trade, health/personal care and prime real estate (Kanyon) provide a demand floor that a cyclical lacks — and disinflation into 2027 mechanically shrinks the TMS-29 monetary loss that has been disfiguring reported earnings.

**Bear case**
- **The operating business has stopped earning.** FAVÖK margin 2.10% in Q1 2026 versus 16.50% in FY2024 — an 87% margin destruction in two years. Operating loss doubled YoY to −₺131.2mn. Gross margin *improved* to 37.9%, so this is an overhead/opex problem management has not yet addressed publicly.
- **Reported profit was always a mark-to-market.** FY2025's ₺2,298mn "profit" rested on ₺5,227mn of investment income. In Q1 2026 that line printed **zero** and the company posted a ₺924mn loss. Strip out fair-value gains and ECILC has been an operating loss-maker for over a year — TTM net −₺640mn, ROE −0.97%.
- **Real contraction plus a discount that isn't special.** Revenue is down ~26% real YoY and ~28% real for FY2025; equity shed ~8.5% real in a single quarter. Meanwhile 0.71× book is *in line with* KCHOL's 0.70 and well above SAHOL's 0.50 — you are not being paid extra to own the weaker business. And on operations alone, EV/EBITDA ≈ 31× is expensive, not cheap.

**Verdict**
ECILC sits in the awkward lower-middle of the Q/P map: **Q = 45** (a fortress balance sheet bolted to a business that has stopped earning) and **P = 39** (only a 5.8% discount to intrinsic fair value, with a bearish MA stack and no confirmed tape either way). The multiplicative blend lands it at **37.5 — Speculative / Weak Hold**: this is not a business in distress and it is not an instruction to sell, but neither the price nor the moment currently pays you to own it, and the modest NAV discount is no wider than what better-run holding peers already offer. **This suits patient, risk-tolerant capital in small size on a 12-24 month horizon, entered on weakness toward ₺61-62 rather than chased at ₺68.30 where risk/reward is 0.24:1.** The stance upgrades to Hold/Neutral on a single concrete event — an operating-profit inflection at the **Q2 2026 print due mid-to-late August 2026**, with FAVÖK margin back above ~8% — and toward Buy only if that is joined by a daily close above **SMA50 ₺78.28 on above-average volume**, which would arm the tape term. It downgrades if Q2 delivers a third consecutive loss or if ₺61.27 breaks on rising volume, which would confirm the freefall pattern that today's higher-low structure still denies.
