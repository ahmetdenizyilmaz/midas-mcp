Judgment: a first-tier franchise plugged into a temporarily throttled end market, with one genuinely rate-independent earnings engine. **Connections = 55.**

## Bull case

- **Cheap on forward book with the down-cycle already guided:** ~1.02x trailing / ~0.9x 2026E PD/DD and ~4.2x 2026E F/K, against a 23-25% ROE guide and 16.4% CAR. The bad news was put in the price by management itself on 28 July; ten brokers cut targets and *kept* Buy, with the lowest target (₺88.80) still 40% above spot.
- **The fee engine is rate-independent and covers 100% of opex.** ₺34.3bn of net fees, +33% YoY, +14% QoQ. Whatever the margin cycle does, the cost base is already paid for — a structural buffer very few banks anywhere possess.
- **The rate lever cuts both ways.** Every TCMB cut from 37% repriices the deposit base faster than the loan book. Management has dated the inflection (late Q3, main effect Q4 2026); disinflation to a 23.95% 12-month expectation is the mechanism, and the market is not paying for it yet.
- **Technically, first credible defence:** a 5-touch ₺61.19 shelf held on +30% volume with RSI washed out at 33.6 — the pattern the backtest says is *mean-reverting* on BIST, not the freefall pattern it says is punished.

## Bear case

- **Real ROE is negative right now.** 19.4% Q2 / 22.2% H1 against a 29.21% year-end TÜFE expectation. Nominal book grew 7% QoQ; real book did not grow. A "0.9x book" argument is worth less when the book itself is losing purchasing power — this is precisely why the valuation score carries a −10.
- **Asset quality is turning, and the leading indicator is worse than the headline.** NPL 3.84% looks contained, but **gross problem-loan inflows doubled QoQ to ₺37.9bn** with cost of risk already at 220bp. Q3 provisions can eat the margin recovery before it reaches the bottom line.
- **Management already missed once.** NIM guidance went 4.0% → 3.2-3.5% and ROE high-20s → 23-25% in a single revision, with the fix pushed to Q4. If TCMB stays paused at 37% through September, that fix slips again — and the sector frame is "repair and rebalancing", not expansion.
- **The chart is not yet on your side:** below SMA20/50/200, MACD bearish, ROC20 −18.7%, −30% from the 52-week high, and immediate resistance ₺0.34 overhead. R:R at spot is 0.83:1.

## Verdict

**AKBNK sits in the awkward middle of the Q/P map: a genuinely good business (Q 57 — top-tier franchise, 16.4% CAR, fees covering all opex) caught in the worst quarter of its margin cycle, at a price that is cheap but not yet *paid* for (P 53 — a real 26% discount, docked for negative real book accretion, and a downtrend that has only just stopped going down).** The multiplicative blend lands it at **54.8 — Hold / Neutral, the "quality watchlist" flavour**: nothing here argues for selling a large, profitable, dividend-paying, deeply liquid bank at 0.9x forward book, and nothing yet argues for buying it at ₺63.40 where the risk/reward to the first real resistance is 0.83:1.

**The horizon this suits is 6-12 months** — long enough to collect the Q4 2026 margin inflection and the next leg of TCMB easing, and to let book compound through the cycle. It does not suit a 4-6 week trade: the tape is neutral (no +7, no −7), the SMA20 at ₺67.88 is unreclaimed, and the near-term ceiling is ₺66.93.

**What moves the stance up:** (1) a daily close above **₺67.88** on at-or-above-average volume — that alone lifts TechnicalTiming toward 70, triggers the +7 tape, and carries FINAL into the low-60s / **Buy**; (2) a **Q3 2026 print (late October 2026)** showing swap-adjusted NIM back above 3.2% *with* problem-loan inflows normalising — that lifts Health and Valuation together; (3) a TCMB cut from 37.0% at the **August or September 2026 MPC**.
**What moves it down:** a loss of the **₺58.72** six-touch shelf on expanding volume (that would complete the freefall pattern, apply the −10 technical penalty *and* the −7 tape, and drop FINAL into the high-30s / Speculative), or a second guidance cut with cost of risk pushing through 250bp.
