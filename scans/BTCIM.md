## Bull case

- **The business generates cash even while reporting losses.** ₺741 mn of operating cash flow and **+₺471 mn of free cash flow in Q1 2026** against a −₺329 mn accounting loss. Under TMS-29 the depreciation on a ₺30.1bn restated asset base is enormous and non-cash — this is not a solvency story, and a rate-cut cycle would convert that cash generation into reported profit quickly.
- **Real asset backing plus a cyclical call option.** ₺26.8bn of equity, ₺30.1bn of PP&E, an **owned port**, energy operations, and a valuable Bornova-İzmir industrial footprint. Cement is a fixed-cost business with violent operating leverage: a modest volume/price recovery as TCMB eases takes FAVÖK from ~₺1.7bn toward ₺2.5-3bn, and net debt is only 21% of equity, so the equity captures nearly all of it.
- **Export + restructuring optionality.** 29% of revenue is export (30+ countries, 4 continents) with the TRY sliding toward a ~51.55 year-end consensus, and the group re-architecture could crystallize value that the current loss-making P&L completely obscures.

## Bear case

- **Priced for the recovery before the recovery exists.** ₺4.90 versus a ₺2.75 base intrinsic value is a **~78% premium**; PD/DD ~1.16 for a business with a *negative* ROE, no dividend since 2016, and a real FD/FAVÖK of roughly 18-21× (the screen's "2.1×" is a duplicated EV/Sales cell, not a real EBITDA multiple). The stock is **+28.6% YTD in TL / +16.4% in USD while losses widened** — that is flow and story, not earnings.
- **The trend is going the wrong way.** The Q1 2026 quarterly net loss (−₺329 mn) exceeds the entire FY2025 loss (−₺252 mn); the operating line went from −₺98 mn (full year) to −₺115 mn (one quarter). Gross margin at 10-12% leaves no buffer, cash is ₺124.6 mn against ₺6.42bn of current liabilities, the current ratio is 0.96, and interest coverage is negative against ₺5.63bn of net debt at a 37% policy rate.
- **Bad moment on the tape, and a dated negative catalyst.** Price is 15.3% below a falling SMA50 and below SMA200, MACD has crossed bearish, ROC20 is −19.5%, and the −30.9% slide from the 52-week high has come on volume 43% above average. ## Verdict

BTCIM sits in the **low-Q / very-low-P** corner of the map: a real, cash-generative, asset-heavy 60-year cement franchise (Health 34, and only that low because it is loss-making at every line with a *widening* trend) that is being asked to support a share price roughly 78% above any intrinsic anchor I can construct — haircut tangible book, peer EV/Sales, or normalized FAVÖK all land between ₺2.00 and ₺4.00, and the market is paying ₺4.90.
