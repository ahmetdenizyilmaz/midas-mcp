*Read-through specific to TURSG:* the 37% rate is a **double-edged** input here and it is the single most important macro linkage in this scan. An insurer's float is invested at policy-linked yields, so today's rate is directly inflating the investment-income line that drives roughly half of TURSG's profit. Disinflation and cuts are a tailwind for the market's multiple and a **headwind for TURSG's own EPS**. Per the brief's instruction, this is reflected in Sector/Health, not by moving the Macro score.

### 2. Sector / industry — Turkish non-life insurance → **62**
**Facts:** TSB reported sector premium production **+28.4% YoY through May 2026**; cumulative sector premium ₺512bn by April 2026; January 2026 alone ₺144bn (+~25% YoY). Health was the leading branch through H1 2026, followed by motor. Sector commentary is explicit that "growth is continuing, but this expansion is being driven by **price increases rather than gaining new insureds**."

**Judgment:** Deflated by ~30–32% YoY inflation, sector premium growth of +28.4% is **roughly flat to slightly negative in real terms** — this is a repricing cycle, not a penetration cycle. Positives: Turkish insurance penetration remains very low versus OECD, giving a long structural runway; MTPL/motor tariff repair over 2024–26 has let the industry write to a profitable combined ratio again; high real rates are currently supercharging float income. Negatives: heavy SEDDK regulation with a track record of tariff intervention on compulsory lines; catastrophe/earthquake concentration; reinsurance cost inflation in TRY; and the structural problem that the sector's current profitability is levered to a 37% policy rate that is on a declining path. A good moment inside an industry whose profit engine is cyclically peaking → **62**.

**FUNDAMENTAL HEALTH → 76** (valuation excluded by construction)
- *Profitability — very strong.* A **combined ratio near 88%** means TURSG is earning a genuine underwriting profit, which is uncommon for Turkish non-life (the industry has historically run 105–115%). - *Balance sheet — strong.* Equity compounding +14.4% in a half-year, comfortably ahead of inflation. Net debt/FAVÖK is not a meaningful metric for an insurer (solvency ratio governs); no FX mismatch or leverage stress flagged; sovereign-fund parentage provides implicit capital backing.

The deduction: a material share of pre-tax profit is investment income on the technical-reserve float, priced off a 37% policy rate on a declining path. A move toward ~25% over 2027–28 would remove a meaningful slice of EPS, only partly offset by underwriting growth.
- *Further deduction:* 76.1% state ownership means pricing on compulsory lines can be directed for policy reasons rather than commercial ones.

Top-decile profitability and a compounding balance sheet, tempered by flat real premium volume and cycle-dependent earnings → **76**.

### 4. Connections / value-chain → **72**
**Facts/judgment:** TURSG distributes through the state banking network — Ziraat, Halkbank and VakıfBank bancassurance — a genuinely privileged and effectively non-replicable channel that underpins the 15% market share. It carries a large share of public-sector and infrastructure risk and participates in state schemes (TARSİM agriculture, DASK-adjacent exposure). End-market is the entire Turkish economy, and compulsory lines (MTPL, DASK) are structurally non-cyclical. Sister company Türkiye Hayat Emeklilik sits under the same TVF umbrella (combined H1 2026 profit ₺24.5bn), giving cross-sell reach into life/pension. Substitutes are minimal; earthquake awareness is a durable demand tailwind.

*The same connection is also the ceiling:* a state-directed distribution advantage comes with state-directed pricing obligations. Scored **72** rather than higher for exactly that reason.

## Bull case
- **Best-in-class underwriting at the #1 franchise.** A ~88% combined ratio (H1 2026) plus ~15% market share means TURSG makes money on the insurance itself, not merely on the float — a distinction most Turkish non-life peers cannot claim, and the reason its ~48.8% annualized ROE beat is durable rather than a one-quarter print.
- **Real value creation, cheaply financed.** ROE of ~49% against ~30% inflation is a +15–18 point real return, and equity compounded +14.4% in six months. Trailing F/K 5.3 is not demanding for a business retaining most of that capital; brokers are anchored far higher (İş Yatırım ₺11.21, 24/04/2026).
- **A distribution moat that cannot be bought.** State-bank bancassurance (Ziraat/Halkbank/VakıfBank) plus public-sector risk mandates, sitting on top of one of the lowest insurance-penetration rates in the OECD — the volume runway is decades long even though it is not showing up in this year's real premium growth.

## Bear case
- **The profit engine is rented from the policy rate.** A large share of earnings is float income priced off 37% rates. TCMB's own survey path (12m-ahead inflation 23.95%) implies materially lower rates through 2027–28; that compresses the exact line that produced the +44% profit growth. The market may already be discounting this — which is precisely why the stock is at 5.3× and not 8×.
- **Real premium volume is flat.** +30% nominal GWP against ~32% inflation is roughly −1.5% real, and the sector itself concedes growth is "price increases rather than gaining new insureds." Margin expansion off an already-88% combined ratio has far less room to run than it did coming from 110%.
- **The cheaper stock is next door, and the float is thin.** ANSGR trades at 3.7× F/K / 1.2× P/B versus TURSG's 5.3× / 2.1× in the same industry; TURSG's ROE justifies most of that premium but not obviously all of it. Meanwhile 76.1% TVF ownership leaves minorities exposed to state-directed pricing on compulsory lines and to dividend policy set by a sovereign fund's needs.

## Verdict
TURSG sits in the **high-Quality / fair-Price** quadrant: Q = 70 is among the stronger business scores this framework produces, but P = 52 says the market has already paid for it — the blended intrinsic base of ₺6.49 is barely 3% above the ₺6.29 close, so the +78% broker target is doing work my own anchors will not support. The technical picture reinforces the "not now" message rather than contradicting it: no freefall and no parabolic risk, but price is pinned between a 2-touch support at ₺6.24 and a **6-touch** resistance at ₺6.4417 on volume 42% below average, which makes the R:R to T1 a poor **0.54:1**. This suits a **12–24 month holder** who wants a compounding, dividend-paying insurance franchise and is willing to sit through the rate-cut earnings compression — not a fresh buyer looking for near-term upside.

**What would change the stance:** (i) a **close above ₺6.4417 on volume at or above the 20-day average** — that alone lifts TechnicalTiming toward 70, triggers the +7 tape term and pushes FINAL into the low 60s, i.e. Buy/Accumulate; (ii) **Q3 2026 results (late Oct/early Nov)** holding the combined ratio below ~92% *while* the policy rate falls, which would prove the earnings are underwriting-driven and justify lifting Health toward 80; (iii) a pullback into **₺5.88–6.06** without fundamental deterioration, which would widen the discount to ~10% and fix the risk/reward. Conversely, a combined ratio drifting back above 100% or a directed MTPL tariff freeze would cut both axes at once.
