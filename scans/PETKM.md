## Block 2 — Sector / industry → 30

Petrochemicals are in the deepest, longest downcycle of the modern era, and Petkim sits on the wrong side of every structural axis:

- **Global overcapacity.** The 2023-2026 Chinese ethylene/polyethylene capacity wave is still landing. Consensus across the industry puts any meaningful spread recovery in **2027**, not 2026 — i.e. beyond the horizon of this scan.
- **Feedstock disadvantage is structural, not cyclical.** Petkim is a **naphtha cracker**. It competes against US ethane crackers and Middle East gas-advantaged capacity. This gap does not close when the cycle turns; it only becomes less painful.
- **No pricing power.** Turkish domestic polymer prices are set at import parity against cheap Asian and Gulf cargoes. Petkim is a price-taker in both directions.
- **The evidence is in the P&L.** FY2025 **gross profit was negative** (−₺3,385mn on ₺98,019mn of sales). A company that cannot make a positive gross margin is not experiencing a soft patch — it is being sold product below cash cost by the global market.

Sector = **30**. Not zero (integration and logistics are real), but a genuinely hostile industry structure with no dated turn.
