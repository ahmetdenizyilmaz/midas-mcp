## Verdict

EUREN sits in the bottom-left quadrant of the Q/P map — a **below-average business (Q 42.7) at an unattractive price and a genuinely bad moment (P 27.6)** — and the two weaknesses compound multiplicatively rather than offsetting. The trap to avoid here is the headline 0.70× PD/DD: a 4.3% ROE against ~29% inflation makes that multiple *fair*, not cheap, and my three independent intrinsic methods converge on a base fair value of ₺3.30 — about 19% **below** the ₺3.93 last price. The horizon that suits this name is long and rate-driven: EUREN is a levered call on the TCMB easing cycle reaching Turkish construction and renovation demand, with a free option on solar glass attached. But that call is not yet in the money and the tape is telling you so.

**What would change the stance:** a Q2 2026 print (~19 Aug 2026) showing gross margin recovering toward 18-20% with *core* operating profit doing the work would lift Health and Valuation together and could move this two bands.
