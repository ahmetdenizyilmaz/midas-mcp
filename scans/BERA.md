## Bull case
- **The balance sheet is real and rare.** D/E 0.28, current ratio 2.95, ₺1.42bn cash, net debt/FAVÖK ~1.8-2.0× — in a 37%-policy-rate year, most BIST industrials would trade at a premium for this. The company can survive a long bad patch without going to shareholders; there is no dilution risk priced or pending.
- **0.27× book with a live market print to prove some of it.** The Golda Gıda IPO (₺9.20, listed 8 July 2026) puts ~₺2.3bn of hard market value on one subsidiary — ~17% of Bera's entire ₺8.7bn market cap — before counting Konya Kağıt, Kompen, Divapan, Adaçal, Anadolu Rulman or ₺7.6bn of book investment property. If management keeps monetising subsidiaries at market prices, the holding discount has a mechanism to close rather than just a hope.
- **Operating businesses are not broken.** 11.2% operating margin on ₺19.1bn FY2025 revenue, genuine vertical integration (wheat→pasta, pulp→decorative paper), 60+ export markets and Romania/Hungary production. The Torbalı integrated energy & steam project attacks the single largest cost line. Fix the financing leak and the earnings snap back a long way from ₺164m.

## Bear case
- **It earns nothing on the book you are buying cheaply.** TTM parent ROE is **−1.30%** against a real cost of equity near 13%. ₺2.15bn of FY2025 operating income leaked ~92% into financing expense and monetary loss to leave ₺164m. A conglomerate compounding at zero real destroys value every year it stays there — and a 0.27× multiple on a shrinking, non-earning book is not a discount, it is a market judgment.
- **The business is contracting in real terms, hard.** Revenue −12/−13% nominal FY2025 ≈ **−34% real**; 9M −17% nominal ≈ −37% real; 9M net profit −54%; Q4 2025 an outright −₺535m loss; **no FY2025 dividend**. The denominator behind the discount is shrinking, which is precisely why the −10 honesty adjustment applies.
- **The tape is in confirmed freefall with nothing beneath it.** Price sits 19% below SMA50 and 26% below SMA200, 2.4% off the 52-week low, −20% in 20 sessions, MACD bearish, and the pivot engine finds **no support level at all** below the current price. The backtest measures this exact pattern at −1.4% excess over 63 days. - **The good news is already spent.** Both hard catalysts — the Golda listing and the Torbalı permit — landed in the first week of July 2026, and the stock is *lower* since. The next dated event is the Q2 2026 report in mid-August, and Q2 2025 was a loss quarter.

## Verdict
BERA sits in the awkward south-west corner of the Q/P map: Quality 44 (a fortress balance sheet dragged under by a bottom line that earns nothing real) meeting Price 41 (a genuine but modest ~27% discount to a ₺17.5 base fair value, comprehensively outweighed by a technical picture in confirmed freefall with zero identified support). The 0.27× book is the loudest number in the file and the least trustworthy one — the FD/FAVÖK of 6.63× and the negative TTM ROE say the earning power is roughly fairly priced and the asset backing is cheap precisely because it earns nothing. This is an asset story with no return on assets, sitting at a 52-week low, having just spent both of its dated catalysts without a bid.

The horizon this suits is long and patient, and the sizing is small — but not yet: buying into an active freefall with no support beneath it, ahead of a mid-August earnings print, is paying for optionality at the worst moment on the calendar. For anyone already holding, the measured path is trimming into strength at ₺14.43 and the four-touch ₺15.17 shelf, not selling into ₺12.78 — there is no urgency here, only a poor moment.

**What changes the stance:** (1) a Q2 2026 print in mid-August showing parent-attributable profit that pulls TTM ROE back above zero — that alone lifts Health and Q toward the mid-40s; (2) the freefall flag clearing — three-to-four weeks holding ≥₺12.48 followed by a close above SMA20 ₺14.19 on ≥7.7m shares, which flips the tape term from −7 to 0 or +7 and mechanically moves FINAL from 31 to the high-30s/mid-40s; (3) a second subsidiary monetisation or the first buyback/dividend, which would convert the NAV discount from a permanent feature into an event. Absent all three, the honest read is that cheap can stay cheap here for years — and it has.
