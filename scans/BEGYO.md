## Verdict

BEGYO sits in the most uncomfortable quadrant of the Q/P map: **weak quality (Q 41, below the 45 speculative line) at a price that — even after a 74% collapse from ₺13.25 — is still not cheap (P 29; roughly 14% *above* a ₺3.04 base fair value).** The instinct that a debt-free REIT at 0.51x independently appraised book must be a bargain is exactly what this framework is built to interrogate, and the interrogation fails on one number: **the portfolio yields 2.95% gross while the risk-free TL rate is 37%.** Capitalising the actual income stream implies under a fifth of the ₺5.5bn book is earning anything at all; the rest is land priced off a discount rate that is currently punitive. What is running is a purchasing-power clock, and it is the more expensive of the two.

The bull case is not empty and deserves to be stated fairly.
