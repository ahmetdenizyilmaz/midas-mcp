### Method 1 (primary sanity check) — Sum-of-the-parts via the listed subsidiary
VESTL consolidates **Vestel Beyaz Eşya (VESBE)**, in which it holds ~93-94%.
- VESBE market cap ₺9,200mn → VESTL's stake ≈ **₺8.6bn**.

**Both cases return negative equity value.** The tell: VESTL's *entire consolidated* equity (₺38.7bn total at YE2025) is barely more than VESBE's *standalone* equity (₺39.0bn) — implying the non-VESBE Vestel Elektronik (the TV / consumer-electronics business) carries **approximately zero to negative** net worth. The group's value is the white-goods subsidiary minus parent-level debt, and that subtraction does not clear.

## Bull case
- **The loss is genuinely narrowing.** Q1-2026 net loss of −₺2.83bn against an implied Q1-2025 of ~−₺6.0bn is a halving. If that trajectory holds through H1-2026, the equity-burn assumption underpinning my ₺22 base case is too harsh and the ₺39 high case comes into view — that is +71% from ₺22.80.
- **No dilution, against the odds.** Paid-in capital is unchanged at ₺335.5mn through a year of ₺32.7bn losses. Existing holders have not been washed out, and the risk overlay carries no bedelli deduction — a materially better position than most distressed BIST names.
- **Real, non-priced optionality.** EV charging, automotive electronics and battery storage are higher-margin businesses with a captive channel through the Zorlu ecosystem, and the SOTP shows the market currently assigns the entire non-VESBE group a negative value. Any of: a decisive TCMB cut reviving credit-financed durable-goods demand, EU trade measures against Chinese appliances, or an asset sale that de-levers the parent, would re-rate an equity stub violently — that is what high leverage does in the up direction too.

## Bear case
- **The capital structure, not the P&L, now decides the outcome.** Net debt ₺100.1bn is **13.1× the market cap** and **6.3-12.5× FAVÖK**. At a 37% policy rate with heavy FX debt, ~₺25bn of financing/FX/monetary losses in FY2025 swamped ₺8-16bn of FAVÖK. The operating business does not earn its interest bill; the equity's fate rests on refinancing terms it does not control.
- **The "cheapness" is an illusion, and the intrinsic work says so twice.** PD/DD 0.29 screens as a deep discount, but SOTP via the listed subsidiary returns **−₺13bn to −₺31bn** of equity value, and peer EV/Sales on any forward revenue run-rate also returns negative. The book is TMS-29-inflated, unrealisable at carrying value, and shrinking ~₺11bn a year. Only the trailing-sales base makes the equity worth anything — meaning the price already assumes the revenue decline stops.
- **The tape and the structure are both broken, with nothing beneath.** Fresh 52-week low on 2026-07-31 (−50.4% from the high), full bearish MA stack, bearish MACD with a widening histogram, RSI at 37 with no oversold cushion, **zero detected supports**, and above-average volume printed *into* the new low. The backtest is explicit that this freefall pattern underperformed by −1.4% over 63 days, and that weak scores are the study's worst cohort.

## Verdict

VESTL sits in the bottom-left corner of the Q/P map — **Q 32 and P 27, weak on both axes simultaneously**, which is the one configuration the multiplicative model punishes hardest and the configuration the backtest identified as its worst-performing cohort.
