Turkish solar generation + project development/EPC.

- **Demand: strong and structural.** Turkish electricity demand grows with GDP and electrification; solar is the fastest-growing segment. YEKA tenders provide a licensed pipeline, and unlicensed/rooftop industrial self-consumption compounds because grid tariffs make behind-the-meter solar attractive.

- **Relative performance:** the BIST renewable/solar cluster is a high-beta, retail-driven complex. MAGEN's own 52-week range — ₺16.576 to ₺68.70 and back to ₺36.90 — is that signature volatility in one line.

Excellent structural demand and falling equipment cost, against bad pricing power and a hostile funding rate → **54**, essentially neutral-plus.

## Bull case

- **The build-out is real and large.** Total assets grew **+23.4% in one quarter** (₺23,052mn → ₺28,443mn, Q1 2026) and none of it is yet in the revenue line. Asset turnover of **0.054×** against a 0.15–0.4× peer norm is the single biggest swing factor in the story: if commissioned megawatts convert, revenue could multiply several times over from a very low base without a single new project being won.
- **Sector tailwinds are genuine and cheap.** Structural Turkish solar demand, YEKA pipeline, electrification/data-centre power demand — and collapsed global module prices that cut the capex cost of every incremental megawatt for a *developer*. A dated new-contract disclosure landed on **2026-07-20**.
- **The chart is basing, not breaking.** The 4-touch shelf at **₺30.535** held, MACD has crossed bullish (histogram **+0.396**), ROC20 is **+12.84%**, and the **freefall pattern is explicitly not active** — the backtest's worst-performing configuration does not apply here. On an 11.7% float, a squeeze needs very little buying.

## Bear case

- **Valuation is the outlier of the entire listed Turkish power complex.** **PD/DD 7.15×** against GWIND 0.8×, AKSEN 1.8×, AYDEM 0.4×, ZOREN 0.2×; **EV/Sales ~76×** against a 1.7–4.5× peer band. Blended intrinsic fair value is **₺2.8 / ₺4.4 / ₺7.2** against a ₺36.90 price — the market is capitalising **₺108.9bn of equity onto ₺28.4bn of assets** producing **₺7.0mn** of quarterly operating profit.
- **The earnings are an accounting artefact and the balance sheet is deteriorating fast.** Q1-2026 net profit of ₺1,445.7mn sits on ₺7.0mn of operating profit (**206×**) — a revaluation/monetary gain, not cash. Meanwhile operating margin fell from **14.5% to 1.8%**, cash fell **56%** to ₺200.6mn, financial debt rose **64%** to ₺8,011.4mn, and indicative interest coverage is ≈**0.01×**. Operations do not service the debt at a 37% policy rate.
- **Real revenue is shrinking and the float invites dilution.** Annualised Q1 revenue is **+1.6% nominal** against restated FY2025 = roughly **−24% real**. Free float is only **11.7%**, so the price is a poor signal of value; and the **2026-07-29 registered-capital-ceiling** filing leaves the door wide open to issuing new shares into exactly this kind of premium.

## Verdict

MAGEN sits in the worst quadrant of the Q/P map: a **weak business (Q 43, dragged down by a Health score of 28) priced as if it were an exceptional one (P 24)** — the geometric blend does what it is designed to do and refuses to let a strong story compensate for both a deteriorating income statement and an eight-fold premium to intrinsic value. The speculative ceiling is technically live (Q < 45) but never gets invoked; at ₺36.90 versus a ₺4.4 base fair value, the price axis alone caps this at 24.7. Note carefully what this rating is **not**: it is not a call that the business is fraudulent or the tape is about to collapse — the freefall pattern is inactive, the ₺30.535 shelf is holding, MACD has turned up, and on an 11.7% float the stock can squeeze violently and stay expensive for a long time.

The horizon this suits is **none at current prices for new money**; for anyone already holding, the measured path is **trimming into strength** at the ₺38.92 / ₺40.4–42.6 / ₺43.04 supply shelf rather than acting with any urgency. Three specific events would change the stance: (1) **Q2-2026 results in mid-to-late August 2026** showing operating margin back toward 14.5% and a profit line that survives stripping the revaluation and the TMS-29 monetary gain; (2) **dated commissioning disclosures** converting the ₺28.4bn asset base into revenue, with a disclosed FAVÖK and net debt/FAVÖK under ~5×; (3) **PD/DD compressing toward the 1–2× that profitable renewable peers command** — whether by book compounding through genuine retained earnings or by price. Absent all three, the honest inverse of the value-trap warning applies: expensive can stay expensive, and a thin float is exactly the mechanism that lets it.
