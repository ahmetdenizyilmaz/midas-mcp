## Bull case
- **Cheap on the least-distorted line.** FD/FAVÖK 2.6x on a ~41% EBITDA margin with net debt/EBITDA of just **0.65x** — the balance sheet is a fortress in a country where leverage at a 37% policy rate is what kills companies. The 20% discount to a ₺125 base rests on EBITDA, the line inflation accounting distorts least.
- **The denominator is not shrinking.** Unlike most cheap Turkish names, equity is rising, real revenue is flat-to-positive, there is no dilution and no loss-making quarter — so no IV-honesty deduction. Cheapness here is more likely to be value than a value trap.
- **Optionality that is actually earning.** Digital services, Paycell fintech and Turkey's largest data-centre footprint give an AI-infrastructure-adjacent re-rating path that a pure mobile utility would not have; İş Yatırım's ₺167.64 target (+68%) prices some of that.

## Bear case
- **A 41% EBITDA margin that becomes a ~7% ROE.** D&A on an inflation-restated asset base plus financing cost at 37% rates consume the operating result. Until real rates fall or the 5G capex cycle rolls off, shareholders capture little of that margin — and low ROE is exactly what justifies a sub-1x PD/DD (the Gordon anchor at current ROE gives ₺94, *below* today's price).
- **The capex cycle is landing now.** Total debt +18% in one quarter (₺174.6bn → ₺206.3bn), a steady bond/sukuk issuance cadence, FX-denominated capex against a lira expected at ~51.55, and management's own guidance conceding 5G margin pressure. FCF is compressed before 5G revenue arrives, and Q1's ~0-3% real growth already ran below the ~8% real guidance.
- **The chart says wait.** Below all three MAs, MACD bearish with a still-widening histogram, ROC20 −7.8%, and an 8-touch resistance only 4% overhead giving 1.01:1 reward-to-risk on the obvious trade. There is no confirmed tape, and the last quarter of news flow was entirely financing — nothing commercial to force a re-rating before 13 August.

## Verdict
TCELL sits on the **high-quality / mildly-cheap-but-badly-timed** corner of the Q/P map: Q 64 is comfortably the better axis, P 54 is held down almost entirely by a 42 technical score rather than by valuation, and R = 1.00 — this is a clean company with nothing in the risk overlay, which is genuinely rare on this board. At 58 it is the top of Hold, two points short of Accumulate, and the missing points are all timing: a 20% discount to a ₺125 intrinsic base is real and the balance sheet earns the benefit of the doubt, but buying 4% below an 8-touch resistance with a still-negative MACD is paying for patience you do not have to pay for.

The horizon this suits is 12-24 months, sized as a core defensive holding rather than a trade — the thesis is that 5G capex rolls off into a lower-rate environment and ROE normalises from ~8.7% toward 10%, which is what moves fair value from ₺94 to ₺116-131. **What would change the stance:** a close above **₺103.95 on above-average volume** lifts Technicals toward 65-70 and arms the +7 tape, printing ~65 → Buy/Accumulate; alternatively **Q2 results on 13 August 2026** confirming the ~8% real-growth guidance with contained 5G margin pressure would raise both Health and Catalysts. Conversely, a decisive loss of the ₺99.27 support (5 touches) opens ₺94.69 and would drag SMA50 below SMA200 — at which point the freefall pattern activates, the −7 tape applies, and this drops into the low 40s. ⚠ Confidence is flagged **low**: the positioning/real-VWAP input was unavailable, İş Yatırım's LTM line items do not fully reconcile (anchored on published ratios instead), and the news sweep rests on the company's own IR/KAP feed rather than a broad press search.
