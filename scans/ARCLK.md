## Bull case

- **The franchise and the footprint are real and now fully owned.** #1 in Turkey, top-3 in Europe; as of 2026-06-19 Arçelik owns 100% of Beko Europe (€71.45m) with production migrating to low-cost Turkey/Egypt/Romania. The heavy lifting — ~1,935 European redundancies, UK/Poland closures, Melano capacity cut — is largely *done*, so H2 2026 should be the first clean read on the savings. Gross margin already improved **+94bp YoY to 29.3%** in Q2.
- **Extreme leverage cuts both ways.** On ₺257.9bn of financial debt, every TCMB cut from 37% flows straight to equity value. If mid-cycle FAVÖK of ~₺43bn arrives at a 7x multiple, the equity is worth ~₺159/share — a triple from here. That optionality is precisely what the 11-broker ₺142.47 average is underwriting.
- **Un-diluted, well-governed, and quietly improving cash.** No bedelli, no SPK measure, no going-concern issue, Koç Holding behind it — and Q2 2026 delivered **positive FCF with lower capex and better working capital**. Whoever is patient is not being diluted while they wait.

## Bear case

- **The "return to profit" is an accounting event.** Strip the ₺6,662m Whirlpool contingent-liability release and Q2 2026 pre-tax was a **~₺3,314m loss**. The seven-quarter loss streak ended on paper, not in the factories. Revenue fell **11.9% in real terms** and FAVÖK margin contracted **168bp to 4.1%**.
- **Leverage is going the wrong way into a maturity wall.** Net debt ₺193.65bn (+7% QoQ), net debt/adj. FAVÖK **5.22x from 4.50x** at YE2025, equity only ₺77.74bn, and **62% of total debt matures in 2026**. At 5x+ leverage a modest FAVÖK miss moves the equity violently — and full-year guidance of 6.25–6.5% requires H2 far above H1's 5.04% in a market shrinking 15% in units.
- **The end market is shrinking, not cycling.** TÜRKBESD H1 2026: total units **−15%**, domestic −7%, **exports −19%**. Three straight years of real revenue contraction, flat-steel anti-dumping tariffs raising input costs, Chinese competitors trading at 5.6x EV/EBITDA taking European share — and **VESBE losing money in both 1Q26 and 2Q26** confirms this is the industry, not the management.

## Verdict

Arçelik sits in the **low-Q / low-P quadrant** — a weak business (Q=39) at a price that is merely *fair* rather than cheap (P=41, base-case discount 5.6%), caught in a confirmed freefall pattern at its 52-week low. The multiplicative blend gives 39.9, the loss-streak deduction takes it to 35.9, and the −7 tape term lands it at **28.9 → Unattractive / Reduce-into-strength**. The market's 0.85x book is not a bargain on a zero-real-ROE business geared 3.3x with an underlying operating loss; the cheapness registers in the model and is then correctly tempered because the denominator — real revenue, equity, FAVÖK coverage — is actively shrinking.

That said, **be honest about how close this call is**: at 28.9 it is 3.1 points below the Speculative band, and the −7 tape is the whole difference. This name is one weekly close above ₺101.5 on decent volume from being *Speculative / Weak Hold*, and a single credible quarter — Q3 2026 printing ≥6.25% adj. FAVÖK margin with net debt falling — from being a legitimate deep-value candidate with 40%+ upside to my ₺132 high case. The Bollinger squeeze at ±2% and the bullish MACD histogram turn say the selling is exhausting, even as the volume says nobody is accumulating yet.

**Horizon and stance:** this suits patient, risk-tolerant capital that can wait 2–4 quarters for a leverage story to resolve, and it suits it *only after* one of the recovery conditions above is on the tape — not before. For an existing holder there is no urgency whatsoever: trim into the ₺107.9–111.3 supply zone if you need to reduce, and note the 1.24:1 reward-to-risk to T1 as the reason not to add here. **What changes the stance:** Q3 2026 margin ≥6.25% (upgrade), net debt/FAVÖK turning down below 4.5x (upgrade), or a reclaim of SMA50 on volume (immediate +7 tape, one full band). **What breaks it further:** a failed 2026 refinancing, a rights issue to fund the balance sheet, or full-year FAVÖK margin landing below 5.5%.
