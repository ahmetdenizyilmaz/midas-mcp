### 2. Sector / industry — **34/100**
Turkish quick-service restaurant / food-service. Demand is discretionary and is the first line item households cut under a real-rate squeeze. Pricing power is weak — consumers trade down within the category and out of it entirely. No FX earnings to offset. Evidence of the squeeze is in the company's own numbers: FY2025 revenue **+47% nominal** but net profit **−74%**.

## Bull case

- **The top line genuinely grows in real terms.** FY2025 revenue +47% nominal vs ~33-35% inflation ≈ **+9% real**, and Q1-2026 sustained it (~+7-9% real). This is a business winning share/traffic, not a melting ice cube — which is what separates it from a true value trap on the fundamentals.
- **Operating leverage cuts both ways, and H1 is the good half.** A food-service operator with a fixed rent-and-labour base and a growing real top line converts violently to profit once cost inflation decelerates — and Turkey's disinflation path (year-end TÜFE expectation 29.21%, 12-month-ahead 23.95%) points that way. Summer seasonality means the ~mid-August H1 print is the best chance of showing it.
- **The chart is not broken and the balance sheet is not distressed.** Price is +26.8% above a rising SMA200 with a golden cross intact, holding a 3-touch floor; net debt/FAVÖK ≈ 1.9× is moderate and equity ₺530.6M is intact. A ₺5.96 reclaim on real volume would add the +7 tape term.

## Bear case

- **You are paying 2.63× book for a business earning ~0% — and now less than zero.** FY2025 ROE ~2.1%, TTM negative, against a 37% policy rate. Every intrinsic anchor except the optimistic recovery case sits **below** ₺5.82; base fair value ₺4.20 implies a **~39% premium**. The Valuation sub-score of 14 is the single largest drag on the score, and it is arithmetic, not opinion.
- **The loss is widening, not stabilising.** Q1-2026 −₺38.9M against a prior-year Q1 loss of ₺9.5M is a **4.1× deterioration**, consuming 7.3% of book equity in one quarter. FY2025 profit had already fallen 74%. The direction of travel on the only line that matters is unambiguous.
- **Structurally the wrong business for this macro.** Pure domestic TRY earner, discretionary demand crushed by a 37% real-rate squeeze, cost base dominated by minimum-wage labour and indexed rent, no FX earnings to offset the TRY slide, no dividend, and repeated VBTS/volatility notices marking it as a flow-driven speculative small cap where the tape can reverse as fast as it arrived (ATR 8.25%/day).

## Verdict

ETILR sits in the least forgiving corner of the Q/P map: **low quality (Q 38) at an unattractive price (P 34)** — the rare BIST small cap that is neither cheap nor healthy. The geometric blend gives it no compensation to work with, the 0.80 risk overlay for VBTS attention and widening losses takes another fifth off, and neither bounded adjustment offers relief: the tape is unconfirmed (volume −38% vs average) and positioning is dead neutral at z = +0.01, meaning the average holder in real lira is exactly break-even with 48% of the year's volume trapped above. **FINAL 28.6 → Unattractive / Reduce-into-strength.** The speculative ceiling of 55 was in force (Q < 45) but never came close to binding.

**For an existing holder, this is a "reduce into strength, with no urgency" file — not an exit alarm.** The primary uptrend is intact, price is holding a 3-touch floor at ₺5.73, and there is a real dated catalyst in the ~mid-to-late-August H1 print that lands in the seasonally strong half. The measured path is to let the stock come to you: stage reductions into the ₺5.96-6.15 supply shelf, then ₺6.48, then ₺6.76-6.91, and treat a close below ₺5.25 (support less 1×ATR) as the signal to reassess rather than to react. What is clearly *not* supported is adding here — R:R to the first resistance is ~0.25:1, a quarter of the 2:1 minimum.

**What would change the stance:** a margin inflection in the H1-2026 results — FAVÖK margin up YoY and the net line back to break-even — would lift Health, remove the earnings deduction (R → 0.90) and move this a full band. A volume-confirmed reclaim of ₺5.96 adds the +7 tape term but, on its own, only carries the score into Speculative. A retreat toward ₺4.20 without further fundamental damage would finally make the Valuation sub-score the argument *for* the stock rather than against it. Absent all three, the score stays where it is — and the risk here is not that cheap stays cheap, but that **expensive gets repriced.**
