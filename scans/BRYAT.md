- **Premium auto distribution & retail** (Borusan Otomotiv 26%, Borusan Oto Servis 20%, Borusan Otomotiv Pazarlama/JLR 26%, Borusan Otomotiv Premium 26%): 37% policy rate + consumer-loan constraints + import/ÖTV cost base = the sharpest cyclical drag in the portfolio. FX-cost / TRY-revenue mismatch on imported units.
- **Construction & mining machinery** (Borusan Makina 29.25% — sole Caterpillar dealer TR + Kazakhstan/Azerbaijan/Georgia/Kyrgyzstan; FG Wilson gensets since 2022): capex-linked, rate-sensitive; the Central Asia franchise and genset/data-centre backup demand are the offsets.
- **Steel pipe** (Borusan Birleşik Boru / BRSAN 9.08%): the bright spot — export-priced energy pipeline demand, TRY slide is a revenue tailwind. BRSAN disclosed progress on the **Gemlik large-diameter pipe facility (KAP, 19-Jun-2026)**.
- **Flat steel & logistics** (Borçelik 15.30%, Borusan Lojistik 30.53%, Supsan 10.02%): mixed; auto/white-goods demand soft, port throughput steady.
- Structural: BIST holding structures are persistently de-rated (management layer, no control premium at the minority level, opacity of unlisted stakes). Relative performance is poor — **BRYAT YTD 2026: −15.99% in TL, −23.98% in USD** (İş Yatırım, as of late-Jul-2026).

## Bull case
- **Fortress balance sheet and irreplaceable franchises:** ₺1.9bn of liabilities against ₺37.6bn of assets, net cash even after a ₺4.67bn payout, and exclusive BMW/MINI/JLR and Caterpillar representations (the latter across four Central Asian markets) that cannot be replicated — genuine option value if TCMB cuts through 30% and big-ticket demand snaps back.
- **The equity-method book almost certainly understates the unlisted stakes.** At 2.0× restated book the SOTP reaches ₺1,729/share — i.e. today's price is defensible if you believe the auto/machinery franchises are worth twice their carried value.
- **Price is at a 5-touch support with volume improving,** 38% off the high, on unusually low volatility for BIST (25.4% annualized) — the setup for a tradable bounce exists even if the investment case does not.

## Bear case
- **You are paying ~19-22× normalized look-through earnings and 1.55× post-dividend book for a 7-8% nominal ROE — while TL deposits pay ~40%.** That is the whole thesis against it; the real ROE is deeply negative and no reasonable cost-of-equity framework justifies a premium to book.
- **FY2025's ₺4.64bn (+128%) was a divestment gain, not earnings power.** Recurring profit is *lower* than 2024's in the same purchasing power, Q1-2026 fell 65% in real terms, and the giant dividend that flattered the yield was a one-time return of the CEVA proceeds — the 2027 payout will be a fraction of it.
- **Structurally disadvantaged minority:** 9-30% non-controlling stakes, no consolidation, 100% related-party revenue, intifa senetleri skimming 5% of distributable profit, a 15.4% float that twice tripped BISTECH circuit breakers in July 2026, and a controlling family holding quietly buying shares from within.

## Verdict
BRYAT sits in the awkward north-west-to-south-east corner of the Q/P map: a **mediocre-to-decent business quality score (Q 52)** built on a fortress balance sheet and world-class distribution franchises, paired with a **poor price score (P 29)** — a ~22% premium to base intrinsic value on shrinking real earnings, and a chart that is below every falling moving average, 38% off its high and 3.7% above a fresh 52-week low. This is the mirror image of the usual BIST setup: not a cheap company with a bad chart, but a **dear one with a bad chart**, which is why the geometric blend lands at 35.8 — Speculative / Weak Hold — despite there being nothing wrong with the balance sheet. It suits only patient money already positioned, sized small, with a multi-year horizon that explicitly bets on a TCMB easing cycle reviving BMW and Caterpillar demand. **The stance changes on three concrete things:** a Q2/Q3-2026 print showing equity-method income inflecting upward, a reclaim of the ₺1,895 SMA50 on above-average volume that ends the lower-lows sequence, or a corporate action that crystallises the unlisted stakes above equity-method book. Until then, the ₺1,766-1,836 band is a trim zone, not an entry.
