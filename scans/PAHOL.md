## Verdict
PAHOL sits in the worst quadrant of the Q/P map: **mediocre quality (Q 43) at an unattractive price and a broken moment (P 33)** — and, critically, it is not the classic "bad company, great price" setup where cheapness earns its keep, because at 0.61× book it is merely at the holding-sector median rather than genuinely discounted. The debt-free balance sheet is real and is the reason this is a *reduce-into-strength* rather than a distressed situation: there is no solvency clock, no dilution pending, no regulatory measure. But a fortress balance sheet holding assets that produce ₺72.3mn of quarterly revenue against ₺43.3bn of equity is a warehouse, not a business, and the Q1 2026 loss showed what the reported earnings power actually was once the revaluations stopped.

**Horizon:** this suits patient, risk-tolerant capital only, and even then not yet — the timing is objectively terrible (freefall active, no support beneath, fresh 52-week lows on declining volume), and the honest observation is that a NAV holding at 0.6× book can sit there for years. Holders should use rallies into ₺1.41-1.47 and ₺1.55 to lighten rather than selling at the 52-week low; buyers have no reason to act at all.

**What would change the stance:** (1) an H1/Q3 2026 print showing profit from *operating* revenue with a real cost base rather than revaluation; (2) a base with ≥2 support touches above ₺1.31 followed by a close above ₺1.47 and SMA20 on above-average volume — that alone removes the freefall penalty and flips the −7 tape, worth 12-16 FINAL points; (3) a resumed TCMB cutting cycle *with* evidence that book value is holding in real terms. Absent all three, the value-trap reading stands.
