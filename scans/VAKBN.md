## Block 4 — Connections / value-chain → Quality 15%

**Score: 58**

- **End market:** the Turkish domestic economy — which policy is deliberately cooling. Loan demand throttled by BDDK caps and 37% rates; this is the core headwind.

- **Substitutes / competitive pressure:** digital-first competition from GARAN and AKBNK, plus participation banks, is eroding the retail moat; VAKBN's digital franchise is competitive but a follower.
- **Value-chain negative:** the same state relationship that supplies cheap deposits also supplies mandated low-margin lending. The advantage and the disadvantage are the same relationship.

## Bull case

- **Cheapest large-cap bank exposure to the Turkish rate-cut trade.** 0.87× book and ~3.8× trailing earnings while earning a 25.7% ROE that beats the 23.9% peer average — the only bank in the peer set with above-average ROE and below-average PD/DD. GARAN and AKBNK earn ~29-30% ROE and command 1.0-1.1× book; closing even half of that multiple gap is worth ~₺3-4 per share.
- **Maximum liability sensitivity = maximum torque on the first cut.** ₺3.43tn of fast-repricing deposits against ₺3.20tn of slow-repricing loans is precisely the balance sheet that suffers most at 37% and gains most on the way down. The September 2026 MPC is the trigger, and İş Yatırım's ₺45.00 target (24-04-2026, +54%) is built on it.
- **Prepared for the credit cycle, and it is a national champion.** ₺2bn of pre-emptive provisions, loan/deposit at 93% with no wholesale-funding dependence, and a state owner with both the will and the demonstrated track record of recapitalising it. Downside is bounded in a way it is not for a private mid-cap bank.

## Bear case

- **The multiple is not a mistake — it is earned.** Equity/assets of 5.9% is thin against private peers as sector NPLs turn up; a 100bp rise in the loss ratio consumes a disproportionate share of that buffer. State ownership means directed, below-market lending campaigns are a permanent tax on margins and the reason the discount has persisted for years rather than closed.
- **Real returns are approximately zero and earnings momentum is negative.** Nominal ROE ~25% against ~29% expected inflation is a real ROE of roughly nil; Q1 2026 profit fell 41% sequentially from Q4 2025, and real YoY growth is ≈ −5%. İş Yatırım cut its target from ₺53.00 to ₺45.00 in ten weeks. Disinflation additionally erodes CPI-linker income before it delivers the NIM benefit — the pain arrives before the payoff.
- **The tape is against it and the structure is one step from breaking.** Below all three moving averages, MACD bearish, −12.7% in twenty sessions, 4-touch resistance 1.7% overhead, and SMA50 sits just 0.3% above SMA200. A death cross would activate the freefall penalty and the −7 tape term, mechanically dropping this score into the low 40s — Speculative — without a single fundamental changing.

## Verdict

VAKBN sits in the **upper-middle of the Q/P map — respectable quality (Q 58) at a mildly attractive price (P 54)** — which is exactly what a 53 means: a real 17.7% discount to intrinsic value, earned by a bank with above-peer ROE and below-peer multiple, but bought at a poor moment and owned through a governance structure that skims value continuously. This is a **Hold / quality-watchlist** name for an investor with a 12-18 month horizon willing to wait for the rate-cut cycle to do the work; it is a poor swing trade today, because the nearest resistance is 1.7% away and the strict risk/reward is 0.42:1.

**What would move it up a band (→ Buy, 60+):** a close above **₺29.73 on above-average volume** reclaiming the 4-touch ceiling — that alone lifts TechnicalTiming toward 65-70 and puts the +7 tape term in play; H1 2026 results in mid-August showing ROE holding above 25% with NPL formation contained; or the September TCMB MPC resuming cuts. Any two of those together take this into the low 60s.

**What would move it down a band (→ Speculative, low 40s):** the **death cross** — SMA50 (31.84) crossing below SMA200 (31.76), currently 0.3% apart — which activates the freefall penalty and the −7 tape term; or losing the ₺28.88 support on rising volume toward the ₺26.75 shelf; or an H1 print showing NPL formation accelerating against that thin 5.9% equity/assets buffer.
