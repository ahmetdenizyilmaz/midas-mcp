## Bull case

- **Deep asset backing at a widening discount.** PD/DD 0.47 against a ₺145bn equity base and ₺353bn of assets, with housing inventory carried at **cost** rather than fair value — reported book almost certainly understates realizable NAV. The sector's NAV discount is ~17 pts wider than its own 5-year average, which is a mean-reversion setup if rates turn.
- **The operating engine is not the problem — and it is improving.** Gross margin expanded to 33.1% and operating margin to 31.3% in Q1 2026 (from 24.7% / 25.2% a year earlier). ₺25bn of FY2025 operating profit was consumed below the line by financing and monetary loss; that drag is **rate-dependent and reversible**, unlike a broken business model.
- **Structural moat plus state anchor.** TOKİ's ~49.3% stake secures preferential land access, the revenue-sharing model pushes construction risk onto contractor partners, and earthquake-driven urban transformation is a multi-decade demand pipeline. Practical dilution and default risk are near zero — and at ₺1.70bn of daily turnover, a position of any size is exitable.

## Bear case

- **The company destroys real value at current rates.** ROE 3.9% (FY2025) against ~29% expected inflation is a **real ROE near −25%**. Nominal profit is an accounting artifact; shareholders are getting poorer in purchasing power every quarter book value grows +1.2% into 7% inflation.
- **The balance sheet turned in twelve months.** Financial debt went ₺12.7bn → ₺55.3bn (4.4x nominal, ~3.3x real), leaving **interest coverage ~1.1-1.2x** at ~40% funding costs and ₺29.3bn of short-term debt against ₺14.2bn of cash. This was a large bet made at the worst possible point in the rate cycle, and it is currently consuming the entire operating profit.
- **Deterioration is broad and the tape confirms it.** Real revenue −49% YoY, real earnings −66%, an implied ₺3.8bn loss quarter in Q4-2025, price 32% below the 52-week high, full bearish MA stack, MACD widening negative, and volume *falling* on the decline — no accumulation is visible. The freefall pattern historically delivered −1.4% excess over the next 63 days.

## Verdict

EKGYO sits in the **low-Q / low-P quadrant** — and that combination is exactly what the multiplicative model is built to refuse to average away. Quality (44) is held down by a real ROE around −25% and a debt stack that quadrupled into a 37% policy rate; Price (38) is held down not by valuation, which at a ~13% discount is mildly supportive (49), but by a confirmed freefall tape (22) that costs a −12 sub-score penalty and a further −7 after the blend. The discount is real; it is simply not wide enough to pay for standing in front of the decline, and with book value slipping ~5.5% in real terms per quarter the discount itself is being quietly eroded.

This is **not a call to dump the stock into a 52-week low** — that is the worst possible execution of a correct view. For a holder, the measured path is trimming into strength toward the 8-touch ₺20.04 shelf, which sits conveniently at the moving averages. For a non-holder, this is a watchlist name with a hard trigger, not a purchase: EKGYO is a legitimate long-horizon asset-backed franchise with a state-anchored land pipeline that is being run through the wrong half of the rate cycle with too much leverage.

**What changes the stance, precisely:** a reclaim of SMA50 ₺19.85 on ≥136mn shares with a bullish MACD cross (worth ~+12 FINAL on unchanged fundamentals, moving it to Speculative/Hold); a Q2-2026 print (~mid-August) showing net profit up YoY with real revenue stabilizing; and above all a TCMB cut that lets ₺25bn of operating profit stop being eaten below the line. Two of those three would move this into genuine Hold territory, and all three would make it a Buy candidate. Until at least one appears, **cheap can stay cheap** — and this one is only 13% cheap.
