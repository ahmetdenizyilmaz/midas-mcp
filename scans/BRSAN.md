## Bull case

- **$1.3bn of contracted US backlog** ($742m June-2026 + $553m Dec-2025) gives revenue visibility through Q1 2028 that almost no BIST industrial can match — roughly 0.75x annual revenue already locked, converting mainly in **2027**.
- **The transformation is real and validated by cash:** Berg Pipe cost $162m in 2023 and has since booked a single order worth 3.4x that. Producing inside the US tariff wall turns Section 232 from a threat into a moat.
- **Balance sheet de-risked:** net financial debt down to **$178m**, leverage **2.8x → 1.3x**, and USD functional currency at subsidiaries closes the historic FX mismatch. Add the Gemlik project's **+50-100bp permanent margin** and **$30m working-capital release**, and 2027 FAVÖK of $200m+ is credible rather than heroic.

## Bear case

- **Valuation offers no cushion.** 12.1x 2026E EV/FAVÖK and 13.2x trailing, against BIST steel at 5-6x and global line-pipe peers at 4-7x. PD/DD **1.88** versus EREGL 1.05 / ISDMR 1.09 / KRDMD 0.51 — a doubled multiple on a **4.65% ROE**. All three intrinsic methods land at ₺308-627 with a base of ₺470, *below* the ₺521.5 price.
- **Returns are far below the cost of capital.** ROE 4.65% nominal against ~29-31% inflation and a 37% policy rate is a deeply negative real return. Margins are thin (7.4% FAVÖK) and earnings lumpy — Q4 2025 was a **₺262m loss** inside a profitable year. No dividend, because statutory records showed a **₺1,034.8m loss** for 2025.
- **The insider sold, and the tape agrees.** Borusan Holding placed 6% of capital at **₺540** in May 2026; the stock is now **below** that price, −33.9% from its 52-week high, under all three moving averages with a bearish MACD, and Friday saw **+65% volume on a down day closing at the lows**. The 90-day lock-up expiring mid-August 2026 keeps the overhang live.

## Verdict

BRSAN sits in the **decent-quality / poor-price** quadrant: Q 57 is respectable — a genuinely de-levered balance sheet, a validated US strategy and $1.3bn of contracted backlog — but P 41 says the market already paid for all of it and then some, at 12.1x 2026E EV/FAVÖK and 1.88x book on a 4.65% ROE. The geometric blend does exactly what it is designed to do here: a good business at a bad price prints **44.7**, not a Buy.

This is a **watchlist name for patient, 12-18 month money**, not a position to open at ₺521.5 with resistance 1.6% overhead and the only real support 4.6% below. The horizon that matters is **2027**, when the Berg Pipe orders convert to revenue — but you should not have to pay a 2027 multiple in August 2026 to own that.

**What changes the stance:** a Q2 print on **7 August 2026** confirming a second profitable quarter with FAVÖK margin ≥8% (lifts Health and Catalysts → high-40s), a reclaim of **₺561 (SMA50) on above-average volume** (lifts Technicals from 33 toward 60-65 → Hold at ~52-55, with a +7 tape reachable above 70), or a pullback into the **₺497-505** four-touch shelf (which would put price at a genuine discount to the ₺470-plus base FV and lift Valuation materially). Any two of the three together would print a Buy. Conversely, a Q2 miss or a further Borusan Holding placement after the mid-August lock-up expiry would remove what little support the ₺521 level has and open the ₺470s.
