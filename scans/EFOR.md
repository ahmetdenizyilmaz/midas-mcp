**Honest counterweight:** expensive can stay expensive far longer than a model expects, particularly on a heavily-churned BIST mid-cap with a 60.7% controlling holder and ₺1.16bn of daily turnover. This scan is emphatically **not** a short thesis and **not** an instruction to sell. But the inverse of the value-trap warning applies with full force here: **at ~6.3× intrinsic value, the burden of proof sits on the price, not on the business** — and the parabolic-extension flag is the model's specific, backtest-calibrated warning that this configuration has repeatedly marked local tops.

## Bull case

- **The balance sheet is genuinely clean.** Net debt ₺962.6m against ₺5,220.5m of equity (0.18×) in a 37%-policy-rate economy is a real advantage — the IPO's 30% debt-repayment allocation did its job. EFOR is not forced to refinance into punitive rates, and it has capacity to buy distressed assets while levered competitors cannot.
- **Demand is about as defensive as Turkish consumer demand gets.** Highest per-capita tea consumption in the world, ~30,000 t/yr of capacity and ~10% national share behind a state incumbent. Volumes do not disappear in a recession, and the earnings trend has turned off the FY2025 zero base (₺2.5m → ₺116.3m in one quarter, a 3.1% net margin).
- **The tape is, factually, still constructive.** Price above all three MAs, SMA200 reclaimed, MACD bullish with an expanding histogram, ROC20 +39%, and a 3-touch support just 2.2% below. Reflexivity is real, and on a name with ₺1.16bn of daily turnover and a 60.7% controlling holder, flows can extend a move well past what fundamentals justify — the ruleset's own core premise.

## Bear case

- **The business destroys value in real terms.** FY2025 net profit of **₺2.5m on ₺13.6bn of revenue** (0.02% margin) is a rounding error. Nominal ROE of 8.9% against a 37% policy rate and ~29% inflation is a large negative real return, revenue is down ~15-18% real, and book value fell ~3-4% in real terms in Q1-2026 alone — the denominator behind that 8.65× PD/DD is shrinking.

## Verdict

EFOR sits in the model's most uncomfortable quadrant: **mediocre quality (Q 44) meeting an unusually poor price (P 32)** — and unlike the classic "great business, bad moment" case, there is no quality here to wait for.
