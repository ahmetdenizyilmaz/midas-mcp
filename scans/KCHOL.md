Tailwinds: rearmament, aviation/jet fuel, disinflation → rate cuts → bank + domestic durables recovery, weak TRY for exporters.
Headwinds: European auto and white-goods recession, Chinese OEM competition, EV substitution risk to ICE commercial vehicles, CBAM/emissions cost creep on refining.

**Judgment: 62** — a genuinely deep and diversified chain, levered to precisely the recovery the macro brief describes, but currently pointed into the wrong half of the cycle.

## Bull case

- **A cyclical trough being priced as a permanent state.** 0.70x book, a 3.4% real ROE, and European autos plus white goods both in recession — this is what the bottom of a cycle looks like on a franchise that owns Tüpraş, Ford Otosan, Arçelik and Yapı Kredi. Normalize ROE to a mid-cycle 7% and the base FV is ₺235 against ₺196.9.
- **The most rate-geared blue chip on the exchange.** Yapı Kredi's funding costs, domestic vehicle credit and white-goods demand all move on the same lever. With the July 2026 pause and year-end TÜFE expectations at 29.21%, a resumed cut cycle hits three Koç segments at once.
- **The technical structure is intact and cheap to be wrong in.** Full bullish MA alignment, confirmed golden cross, +6.2% above the 200-day, RSI 52.7 with room, and 27.5% annualized volatility — this is a low-drama chart. The ₺3.54 bn daily turnover means position size is never the constraint.

## Bear case

- **The earnings trend is not merely weak, it is deteriorating.** ₺24.2 bn FY2025 → **₺522 mn in Q1 2026** on ₺494 bn of revenue — a 0.11% net margin. Nothing in the macro brief (37% policy rate, ~29% year-end inflation) says Q2 was better, and H1 results land within days of this scan.
- **A 3.4% real ROE does not compound.** Book value is growing far below cost of equity, so the 0.70x PD/DD is cheap against a denominator that is drifting rather than building — hence the −10 IV-honesty deduction. Cheap can stay cheap for as long as the cycle stays where it is.
- **The tape is not confirming and the ceiling is close.** Friday's +2.82% came on volume 7.2% *below* the 20-day average, MACD has crossed bearish, and ₺203.65 has held five times. R:R to T1 is 1.34:1 — you are being asked to pay for the recovery before the tape has voted for it.
- **Opacity where it matters.** FD/FAVÖK is A/D, holding-only net debt cannot be separated from Yapı Kredi's consolidated bank balance sheet, and three months of KAP filings went unverified in this scan.

## Verdict

KCHOL sits in the awkward middle of the Q/P map: **Quality 52** — a world-class franchise reporting genuinely poor numbers (3.4% real ROE, 1.2% net margin, Q1 2026 profit of ₺522 mn) — against **Price 57**, where a 16% discount to a ₺235 intrinsic base is real but tempered by the fact that book value is drifting rather than compounding. The geometric blend lands at **55, the top of Hold**, one point short of Buy/Accumulate, with a clean risk overlay (R = 1.00, no deductions of any kind) and no tape adjustment in either direction. This is not a stock with a problem; it is a great business at an unresolved moment.

The horizon this suits is **12-24 months, and it is a rate-cycle trade, not a stock-picking trade.** Koç is the highest-beta blue-chip expression of Turkish disinflation — Yapı Kredi, domestic auto credit and white-goods demand all inflect together when TCMB resumes cutting. Buying it before that inflection means paying for patience while trough earnings keep printing.

**What moves the stance up to Buy/Accumulate:** (1) H1 2026 results — due within days — showing the Q1 collapse was a trough rather than a trend, i.e. quarterly profit re-establishing a run-rate consistent with a mid-single-digit real ROE; (2) TCMB resuming cuts at the August or September 2026 MPC, which lifts Sector, Health and Catalysts simultaneously; (3) technically, a close above **₺203.65 on above-average volume**, which would take Technicals through 70 and — with volume confirmation — trigger the **+7 tape adjustment**, mechanically carrying FINAL into the low 60s.

**What moves it down:** H1 results confirming margin compression rather than trough, a loss of the ₺190.20 support shelf (which would put price below SMA50 and start the freefall clock), or any capital-raising or governance disclosure emerging from the KAP window this scan could not verify.
