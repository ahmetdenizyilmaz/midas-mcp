## Bull case
- **The sector is on the right side of the decade and GESAN is vertically integrated into it** — turnkey EPC *plus* its own HV/MV/LV manufacturing, engineering and testing, delivering into a global transformer/switchgear shortage across 80 countries, with a TRY tailwind on the export book.

If the stake is verified at 40-50%, the listed holding alone covers 65-82% of GESAN's market cap and the international EPC franchise plus the 2+GW solar / 3.5+GW wind pipeline come close to free — a ₺95-115 fair value.

## Bear case
- **The earnings are low-quality and getting worse:** net margin 5.8% → 4.4% → **2.7%**, ROE **7.1% falling to 4.7% annualised** — roughly a third of ASTOR's 18.9% and EUPWR's 14.1%. Nominal profit growth of +22% against ~35% inflation is a **real decline**. At 2.24× book on a 7% ROE, GESAN is not obviously cheap on its own merits — only against peers who may themselves be the mispriced ones.
- **Growth is being bought with the balance sheet, not earned in cash:** financial debt +77% in 15 months (2,873 → 5,091) while cash *fell* to ₺483mn; assets grew ₺3.8bn in a single quarter. Estimated interest coverage ~1.8× against a **37% policy rate** is the tightest link in the chain, and real book value per share is eroding ~15%/yr.

## Verdict

GESAN sits in the **middle-middle of the Q/P map — Q 55, P 45** — a decent-not-great business at an unremarkable price in an excellent sector: the multiplicative blend gives it no lift from either axis, and with no tape to add (TechnicalTiming 46, volume 55% below average) it lands at **46.8, the defensive edge of Hold**. The core tension is that the two things this stock has going for it — a real-growing top line and a P/B less than half its peers' — are both undercut by the same fact, a **4.7-7.1% ROE with margins compressing at every line and growth funded by debt rather than cash**; a low P/B on a low ROE is arithmetic, not a discount. **This suits patient capital waiting for a level, not money that needs to own it today** — at ₺74.75 the risk/reward to the first resistance is 0.63:1, while at ₺67-69 the same trade is 2.82:1.

Two specific events would change the stance in either direction. **Upward:** the H1 2026 report due ~late August showing net margin recovering toward 4.4% with positive operating cash flow, or a reclaim of **₺82 on volume at or above the 20-day average** (which alone unlocks the +7 tape and carries FINAL to ~59-61); verification of a 40%+ EUPWR stake would independently reset fair value to ₺95-115. **Downward:** an H1 print confirming the 2.7% net margin as structural rather than seasonal, or a daily close below **₺63.30** — beneath which there is no seriously tested support until ₺53.65, 28% lower.
