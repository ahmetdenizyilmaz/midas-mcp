- **Demand:** discretionary apparel is the first line item cut when real wages compress. The 37% policy rate and TL deposit competition (Block 1) pull household spending out of shopping and into deposits. Apparel/textile has lagged XU100 through the tightening cycle.

- **Input costs / FX:** cotton and imported inputs are FX-linked; the controlled TRY slide (USD/TRY ≈ 51.55 expected year-end) raises COGS faster than domestic pricing can follow. Partly offset by MAVI's international (US/Germany/Canada) revenue, which is an FX earner.
- **Regulation:** no sector-specific regulatory overhang identified.

**Judgment: Sector = 45.** Below neutral. Cyclically this is the wrong end of a high-real-rate economy, and structurally the low-price tier is under permanent cross-border attack.

- **Structural, not just cyclical, competition:** Shein/Temu/Trendyol private label at the entry price point do not retreat when rates fall.

## Verdict

MAVI sits in the awkward north-west of the Q/P map — a **good-quality business (Q 58) at a poor price and a poor moment (P 39)**, which the multiplicative blend correctly resolves to **46.8 → Hold/Neutral** with a clean risk overlay (R = 1.00) and no tape adjustment either way. This is a genuine quality-watchlist name rather than a value opportunity: the ₺3.88 bn net cash position, 21.9% EBITDA margin, 4.27% dividend and active buyback are all real, but at ₺39.12 the shares trade at a **~10% premium to a ₺35.7 base-case intrinsic value that is itself declining in real terms** — and the stock is the most expensive of its listed Turkish peers on both F/K and PD/DD.

The horizon this suits is **12-24 months and macro-dependent**: MAVI is a high-torque call option on the TCMB easing cycle, and it should be owned when that cycle resumes, not before.
