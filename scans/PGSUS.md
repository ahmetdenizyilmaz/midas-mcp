The industry-wide Pratt & Whitney GTF engine-availability problem is an ongoing overhang for heavy A320neo operators, constraining ASK growth and raising unit costs (treated here as a standing sector overhang — no fresh dated Pegasus-specific disclosure retrieved).

Relative performance: **−20.19% over 12 months** (Mynet, 2026-07-31) and **−44.26% from the 52-week high** — a clear underperformer against XU100.

Net: structurally attractive positioning, currently in the down-leg of the cost/demand cycle. Marginally above neutral.
