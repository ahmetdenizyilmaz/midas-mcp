## Bear case
- **The price already assumes the bull case twice over.** F/K 29.6 and PD/DD **21.8** (2026-07-31) against a peer group of BIST financials at 5-10× earnings and 1-2× book. Even the ROE-identity anchor, run on a 116% ROE with generous 35% growth, tops out around ₺83. Base-case fair value ₺95 implies the stock is **~64% above intrinsic value**.
- **Growth may already be flattening.** Implied Q4 2025 net profit ₺3.34bn versus Q1 2026 ₺3.36bn — flat nominally, a real decline at 30% inflation. A 30× multiple has no tolerance for a decelerating quarter, and Q2 results land within weeks.
- **Structural fragilities the multiple ignores:** a queue-model business that depends on continuous new-saver inflow, a single BDDK regulatory point of failure, an eight-year operating history with no full cycle, a 23.2% free float that amplifies moves in both directions, and an unresolved 238% capital action. ## Verdict
KTLEV sits in the awkward top-left of the Q/P map: **high quality (Q 69) at a demanding price (P 36)** — the mirror image of the usual BIST value trap. The business is not the problem; the multiple is. At F/K 29.6 and PD/DD 21.8 the market has already paid forward several years of the growth this franchise is delivering, and the technicals confirm that the pay-forward peaked on 2026-07-16 — price is now below SMA20 and SMA50 with a bearish MACD, though critically **not** in freefall (SMA50 sits 122% above SMA200) and with selling volume drying up 54% below average.
