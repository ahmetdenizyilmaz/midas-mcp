## Bear case

- **You own nothing directly, and the discount you are "buying" is the market's correct price for that.** Every lira of value is second-hand: a ≈44.7% non-controlling stake, inside a chain where **TRENJ owns 52.25% of you**. BIST holding discounts widen as easily as they narrow, no dated catalyst exists to close this one, and a 37% policy rate — which makes idle holding-company assets expensive to own — historically *widens* them. **The 30% discount is compensation, not a coupon.**

## Verdict

TRMET sits **mid-map — Q 57, P 58, both a whisker above neutral — and lands at 51.9, the middle of Hold.** The underlying asset is genuinely good (net cash, ~55% FAVÖK margins, +93% real revenue growth, a dated four-mine ramp) and the wrapper is genuinely cheap-looking (a 30% discount to NAV, look-through 2026E F/K of 9.3x versus the sister's 12x). What stops it becoming a Buy is that the discount is **earned rather than anomalous**: a single-asset, non-controlling, twice-subordinated holding vehicle with a TMSF-trustee history is *supposed* to trade 25-40% below NAV, and at 30% it is squarely inside that band. Score the wrapper honestly and the stock is fairly valued — 2.0% below a ₺127 base fair value.

This suits **patient, risk-tolerant money on a 12-24 month horizon that wants leveraged Turkish gold exposure and is content to be a passenger** — but there is no case for paying ₺124.50 today when a 3-touch shelf with SMA50 confluence sits at ₺114.47 and risk/reward from spot to T1 is 0.12:1. **Accumulate ₺114.50-118.00, stop ₺107.56, underwrite T2 at ₺142.67 rather than T1.** Note the pair trade the numbers imply: if you want these ounces and can tolerate the structure, TRMET is the cheaper access point; if you want control, dividends and a cleaner story, TRALT is worth its 12x.
