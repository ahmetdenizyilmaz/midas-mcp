## Bull case

In a 37% policy-rate regime this is a decisive structural advantage: REEDR's peers are paying punitive interest into a demand recession while REEDR is not. It buys time that the peers do not have, and it means no forced bedelli.
- **Branded economics, not assembler economics.** 47.6% FY2025 / 43.8% Q1 2026 gross margins, Brand Finance's 3rd most valuable Turkish device manufacturer (2025). The problem is opex and monetary loss, not the product's ability to earn a spread. Fix the cost line and the operating leverage on ~45% gross margins is substantial — and the operating line has *already* turned positive in Q1 2026, with the net loss narrowing ~20% YoY.
- **The last capital increase was bedelsiz, not bedelli, and the diversification is real.** No dilution risk on the record (2025-09-23, 300% bonus); ~63-66% free float with ₺98.6m daily turnover. Meanwhile the product base has widened into e-mobility with actual production (golf carts, April 2026) and an actual signed export contract in USD-equivalent cash terms — the beginning of a fix for the FX mismatch.

## Bear case
