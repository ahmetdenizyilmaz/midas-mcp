## Bull case

- **Backlog $334M = 3.3× FY2025 revenue**, up 67% in three months, anchored on KAAN, HÜRJET and Roketsan — the exact programs Turkey is funding through 2033. Revenue visibility here is far better than the Q1 print suggests.

- **Cheapest defense name on the exchange by a wide margin** — PD/DD 3.06 vs a 4.26 sector arithmetic average and 5.48 for ASELS; FD/FAVÖK 20.8 vs 44.2 sector. If Q2 confirms the inflection, the multiple re-rating alone is worth more than the earnings recovery.

## Bear case

- **Three consecutive years of falling profit, now a loss quarter, with ROE ~3.6% against a 37% policy rate.** The company earns less on shareholder capital than a TL deposit account, and Q1 interest coverage was below 1×.
- **The 2026 guidance requires ~$88M of revenue in nine months after delivering ~$13M in the first three.** Management reaffirmed it unchanged and deferred the reckoning to Q4 — that is a credibility bet, not a forecast, and the Q2 print in ~3 weeks settles it.
- **The cheap multiple is only cheap against a bubble.** Sector FD/FAVÖK of 44× is not a valuation anchor; on cost-of-capital math (justified PD/DD ~1.8-2.2× on a ~10% normalized ROE) fair value is ₺9-13, *below* today's ₺15.38.

## Verdict

ALTNY sits in the awkward quadrant of the Q/P map: **decent-not-great quality (Q 59) at an unattractive price and a poor moment (P 38)** — good business, wrong entry, wrong time. The multiplicative blend prices that honestly at 43.6, a hair under the Hold band, and it gets there without any hard penalty firing: no freefall (the golden cross is intact), no parabolic flag, no ceiling, and only a −0.05 risk deduction for the loss quarter. What holds the score down is arithmetic, not prejudice — a stock 14% above its intrinsic base case, with declining intrinsic value, below all three moving averages, three weeks ahead of the earnings print that decides whether the 2026 guidance was real.

This suits **risk-tolerant money on a 12-24 month horizon and a small position**, and the sensible way to own it is to let the market answer the question first. The stance moves up on a Q2 print showing revenue back above ~$25M with a return to profit, or on a volume-backed reclaim of ₺15.87 and then the ₺16.35 SMA50 — either would add roughly ten points and put ALTNY solidly in Hold, and both together would make it a Buy. It moves down if Q2 misses again, if guidance is finally cut, or if price loses the 8-touch ₺14.36 shelf and drags SMA50 under SMA200 — at which point the freefall penalty and the −7 tape both activate and the score falls into the twenties.
