## Bull case
- **Genuinely cheap on every intrinsic anchor at once:** 8.4× earnings, **0.65× book**, 4.0× FD/FAVÖK, 0.6× sales, with a **6.64% cash dividend actually being paid**. This is a profitable, ₺123bn-revenue category leader — not a speculative shell — and the market is pricing a 7.7% ROE as permanent. Any reversion toward the 11.3% ROE ULKER earned in FY2025 mathematically re-rates the book multiple from 0.63× to ~1.0×, i.e. ₺84 → ₺137.
- **The macro turns in its favour on a lag.** ₺41.1bn of net debt at a 37% policy rate is the earnings anvil; the July 2026 MPC pause sits inside a disinflation path (year-end TÜFE expectation 29.21%, 12-month-ahead 23.95%). Each 100bp of eventual easing is direct, high-torque earnings leverage — and a squeezed consumer recovering is the second leg of the same trade.
- **Structurally supported downside.** Sell-side is far above (İş Yatırım AL ₺209.26 dated 24 Apr 2026; consensus range ₺126-171), foreign ownership of the float is 73.72%, and annualised volatility is only 19.77% — this is an orderly institutional de-rating in a liquid large cap, not a small-cap collapse. RSI 18.5 below the lower Bollinger is the kind of washout that, on BIST specifically, the backtest found mean-reverts.

## Bear case
- **The denominator is genuinely shrinking.** TTM net profit ≈ ₺3.7-3.8bn vs FY2025 ₺5,363.9m — **−29% nominal**, and under TMS-29 inflation accounting that is close to a real number. Q1-2026 annualised revenue is only +10% nominal against ~29-30% inflation, i.e. a real top-line contraction. "0.65× book" means little if book earns 7.7% and falling — this is the classic value-trap shape, and it is why I docked Valuation by 10.
- **The tape is broken with nothing beneath it.** The technical engine returns **zero supports** — every swing pivot has been taken out. Price is at a new 52-week low, below all three MAs with SMA50 < SMA200, MACD bearish and *widening*, on volume 47% above average. There is no level to lean on; the stop in the Levels section is a line drawn on empty chart.
- **Leverage into a cost squeeze.** Net debt/FAVÖK ~2.3-2.4× with hard-currency borrowing against a TRY heading toward ~51.55, while cocoa/palm/wheat inputs are FX-linked and the consumer will not absorb full pass-through. The margin math gets worse before the rate cuts arrive — and the Q2-2026 print (~19 August) is the next place that shows up.
- **Unverifiable recent disclosure record.** KAP, the IR site and the news aggregators were all unreachable this scan. I am rating the News/Governance block near neutral on incomplete information; a bedelli, a guidance cut or a material related-party item in May-July 2026 would not have surfaced here.

## Verdict
ULKER sits in the awkward quadrant of the Q/P map: **middling-but-real quality (Q 55) at a genuinely cheap valuation (28% discount to a ₺120 base) attached to one of the worst tapes on the board (Technicals 20).** The multiplicative blend does exactly what it was designed to do — it refuses to let a 28% discount redeem a stock making new 52-week lows with no support beneath it — and the −7 freefall tape drops it from a 45 Hold to a **37.9 Speculative / Weak Hold**. This is not a "sell": it is a "not yet". The horizon that suits it is 12-24 months and the position size is small, because the thesis requires two things that have not happened — an earnings inflection and a price base.

**What changes the stance:** a Q2-2026 print (~19 August 2026) that stops the real earnings erosion, plus a higher low above ₺84.90 and a daily close back above SMA20 (₺94.91) on above-average volume. That combination alone removes the −7 tape and lifts TechnicalTiming into the 45-55 range, which carries FINAL to roughly **52-55 (Hold/Neutral)**; add a first TCMB cut feeding the ₺41.1bn debt stack and normalized ROE recovering toward 11%, and this is a low-60s **Buy/Accumulate** with fair value ₺120-150 against ₺86.30. Until then, it belongs on the watchlist with a written trigger, not in the portfolio. If already held, the measured path is to hold with conditions and add only on the reclaim — not to chase the knife at ₺86 into a print two weeks away.
