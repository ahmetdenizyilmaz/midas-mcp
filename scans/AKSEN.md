**Bull case**
- **The growth is contracted, not hoped for.** 975 MW across 10 projects lands in 2026 alone (Ghana, Kazakhstan, Gabon, Senegal + 251 MW domestic renewables), taking installed capacity to ~4 GW and 5 GW by 2028 — under 20–25 year PPAs with purchase guarantees and **EUR/USD-indexed tariffs**. İş Yatırım's 2027E FAVÖK of **$594mn vs $394mn in 2026** is a +51% step-up that is already financed and under construction.
- **The FX structure is a feature.** ~80% of EBITDA is earned in hard currency against hard-currency debt — so a sliding TRY is a tailwind, not the balance-sheet threat it is for most leveraged BIST industrials. Third-party validation from AFC ($450mn) and a SERV-covered Helaba facility (€57mn, 21-Jul-2026).
- **Leverage is below the sector and the margin is expanding.** Net debt/EBITDA 3.7× against a 4.8× sector average, with the EBITDA margin up to 33% in Q1 2026 from 29%, and İş Yatırım modelling 40.7% for Q2.

**Bear case**
- **The cheapness has already been paid for.** The stock is +200% YTD as of 17-Jul-2026 and +166% off its 52-week low. QNB's 9.9× / 5%-discount-to-peers call was made at **₺79 in April**; at ₺94.15 the same maths is ~11.1× — a premium. F/K 28.4× and PD/DD 1.80× against a ~6% real ROE leave no margin of safety, and my base FV of ₺96 sits ~2% above spot.
- **Real contraction is happening now; the growth is 2027-loaded.** Under TMS-29 these are inflation-adjusted numbers, so Q1 2026's −21% revenue and −11% EBITDA are *real* declines. Domestic revenue fell 45% on weak spot prices and hydrology. Meanwhile financing costs from the capex programme keep suppressing net income — İş Yatırım expects Q2 net profit pressured *despite* EBITDA growth. Any slippage in the 10-project commissioning schedule and 2027 estimates reset downward.
- **Frontier sovereign concentration plus a thin float.** Cash flows depend on state utilities in Burkina Faso, Ghana, Senegal, Gabon, Uzbekistan and Kazakhstan — a class of counterparty with a documented history of tariff renegotiation and payment arrears. With only 19.45% free float against 79.42% Kazancı Holding and 61% annualized volatility, price discovery is thin in both directions.
