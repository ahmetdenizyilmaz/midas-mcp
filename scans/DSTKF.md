## Bull case

- **The business is genuinely good, and that is not in dispute.** H1-26 net profit ₺2,625mn, **+74% nominal / ≈ +31% real** YoY, accelerating q/q (Q2 ₺1,446mn vs Q1 ₺1,179mn), with ROE ~38.8% annualised — all self-funded, no dividend drain, no dilution.
- **Fortress, under-levered balance sheet.** Receivables ₺22.6bn on ₺14.8bn equity (~1.6×) versus GARFA's ~6.5×. There is no funding cliff, no FX mismatch, and real optionality: levering toward peer norms could multiply earnings without a single new customer.
- **The rate cycle is still a tailwind.** With TCMB at 37.0% and consensus year-end TÜFE 29.21%, further easing extends the funding-cost relief that drove the H1 margin expansion — and the VBTS window lapsing 2026-08-20 removes a liquidity constraint.

## Bear case

- **The valuation is not a stretch, it is a different asset class.** PD/DD **38.31** and F/K **116.19** against a listed peer set at **1.0-6.9× book** and **2.9-10.4× earnings**. GARFA earns ₺1.46bn per half on a ₺44bn book for a ₺10.5bn market cap; DSTKF earns ₺2.63bn on a ₺22.6bn book for **₺567.7bn — 54× the market cap for 1.8× the profit.** No plausible growth path bridges that.
- **The profit growth is borrowed from the rate cycle.** Revenue is roughly **flat nominal ≈ −22 to −25% real**; the entire earnings surge is margin, not volume. When funding-cost relief ends or asset yields catch down, growth normalises — and a ~60% annualised gross book yield means the credit losses from this vintage have not been booked yet.
- **The tape and the regulator both say the same thing.** −57.0% from the 52-week high, −37.4% below SMA50, ROC20 −55.3%, MACD bearish and widening, closing at the session low on **−53.8% volume** (distribution ending, but no accumulation starting), with **no multi-touch support until ₺630.5 (−63%)**. ## Verdict

DSTKF sits in the most awkward corner of the Q/P map: **decent quality (Q 54) attached to one of the worst prices on the exchange (P 18)** — the geometric blend is unforgiving precisely because it should be. This is not a distressed company and nothing here reads as a value trap on a shrinking denominator; it is a sound, compounding, conservatively-financed factoring business whose shares are marked at **12.6× my base intrinsic value** after a ~50× post-listing run and a −57% unwind that is still in progress. For a holder, the measured path is **trimming into strength toward ₺1,934-2,200**, sized for a 13.4%-of-price daily ATR, with no urgency implied and no reason to sell into the ₺1,703 low.

The stance changes on evidence, not on the passage of time: it moves to **Hold** if price reaches roughly **₺250-400** (or if book compounds toward it) *while* real revenue growth resumes above inflation and leverage moves toward peer norms; it moves to **Buy** near **₺135-210**.
