### 2. Sector — refining (score **62**)
- **Cycle position, dated:** Brent ran to **~$100** on Iran/US escalation and Strait-of-Hormuz risk (Investing TR, 2026-07-20 and 2026-07-14), then fell back to **~$88 on ceasefire expectations (2026-07-28)**. Analysts had flagged a $120–150 scenario on a Hormuz closure (2026-07-13). Mediterranean product cracks have been wide through 2025–26 on the same tension; İş Yatırım's note (2026-04-09) states "product margins showed resilience in 2025 amid geopolitical tensions".
- **Structural support:** almost no new global refining capacity coming on stream, Turkish domestic fuel demand still growing, and Tüpraş's flexible crude slate (Iraqi/Kazakh/discounted grades) is a durable cost advantage.
- **Structural drags:** refining is one of the most violently cyclical businesses on the exchange — the same headline that widened the crack ($100 Brent) reversed 12% in a week. EU restrictions on refined products of Russian-origin crude are a live compliance/logistics constraint for Turkish exporters. Long-dated: carbon cost and EV penetration erode the gasoline barrel.
- **Judgment:** the environment is currently favourable but **late-cycle and two-way**. İş Yatırım's own numbers embed this: 2027E net profit (₺49.2bn) is *below* 2026E (₺66.6bn). A sector at a good moment, not a good sector. → **62**.

### 4. Connections / value-chain (score **70**)
- **Parent:** controlled by Koç Holding via Enerji Yatırımları A.Ş. — institutional governance, funding backstop, disciplined capital allocation.
- **Downstream:** the dominant supplier to Turkey's fuel distribution chain (Opet affiliation), with jet fuel feeding a structurally growing Turkish aviation complex and naphtha feeding domestic petrochemicals — captive, non-discretionary demand.
- **Upstream:** a flexible, opportunistic crude slate is Tüpraş's real moat — the ability to arbitrage discounted grades is worth more than refinery hardware in most years. The flip side is Hormuz/sanction exposure on that same flexibility.
- **Thematic:** "Energy to the Future" — carbon-neutral by 2050, SAF/biofuel, **416 MW of zero-carbon electricity capacity**, green hydrogen. This is what separates Tüpraş from a stranded-asset refiner narrative, though it is capex-heavy and dilutive to near-term returns.
- **Substitutes:** EV penetration is a slow, long-dated drag on the gasoline barrel; diesel and jet are far more defensible.
- Foreign ownership **47.07%** — an institutional register, which cuts both ways (quality validation, but also a source of flow risk on EM outflows).

## Bull case
- **Balance sheet you cannot buy elsewhere on BIST at this size.** Net cash of ₺74.7bn in a 37%-policy-rate economy means Tüpraş earns on its treasury while leveraged peers bleed on theirs; Moody's affirmed Ba2 on 2026-07-29. It funds an ~80%-payout dividend policy *and* energy-transition capex without touching shareholders — no bedelli risk here, which is rare on this exchange.
- **The forward multiple is genuinely cheap if the estimate holds.** 8.5x 2026E earnings and **4.1x 2026E FD/FAVÖK** on a monopoly-position asset running at 92.6% utilisation, with a 6.6–7.5% dividend yield underneath. Q1 2026 (₺3.71bn) was a trough print, and the H1 result due in days is the first chance to show the inflection.
- **Geopolitics is currently paying the owner.** Constrained Hormuz passage and tight global refining capacity keep Mediterranean cracks wide; Tüpraş's flexible crude slate turns that dislocation into margin.

## Bear case
- **You are paying roughly fair value after a 100.7% run.** Base intrinsic value is ₺285 against ₺295.25 spot — a −3.6% "discount". The stock sits 7.8% below its 52-week high, the MACD has already crossed bearish, and volume is 15.3% *below* its 20-day average — the advance is not being confirmed by participation. **Risk/reward at spot to the 52-week high is 0.45:1.**
- **Consensus itself sees 2026 as the peak.** İş Yatırım models ₺66.6bn for 2026 and **₺49.2bn for 2027** — a −26% earnings step-down. Buying a cyclical at 8.5x peak-year earnings is buying ~11.6x the following year, and the year after that is a coin flip on cracks.
- **The margin is a headline away from halving.** Brent went from $100 to $88 in a single week on ceasefire *expectations* (2026-07-28). The trailing payout ratio is already 120% of earnings, so a prolonged trough would force either a dividend cut or a drawdown of the very net-cash position that justifies the quality premium. Add EU restrictions on Russian-origin refined product and 47% foreign ownership as a flow-risk amplifier.

## Verdict
TUPRS sits in the **high-quality / fairly-priced** quadrant of the Q/P map: Q = 66 is one of the better business scores available on BIST — net cash, 92.6% utilisation, Ba2, Koç governance, zero risk-overlay deductions (R = 1.00) — but P = 56 says the market has already collected most of the near-term reward, with the stock +100.7% off its low, momentum rolling over on below-average volume, and price ~4% *above* a ₺285 base fair value. The resulting 60/100 is a **Buy/Accumulate at the very bottom of the band — an accumulate-on-weakness rating, explicitly not a chase-here rating**, and the 0.45:1 risk/reward at ₺295.25 is the number that makes that concrete. It suits patient income-plus-cycle money on a 12–24 month horizon, sized to tolerate 39% annualized volatility.

**What would move the stance up:** an H1 2026 print in the first half of August that validates the ₺66.6bn full-year path (i.e. a Q2 net profit materially above ₺15bn), *plus* a reclaim of ₺320 on volume **at or above** the 20-day average — that combination would lift TechnicalTiming through 70, trigger the +7 tape term, and carry FINAL into the high 60s. A pullback into ₺251–256 (support 251.5 + SMA50 253.3) that holds would do the same job from the valuation side, opening a ~12% discount to base FV and a 6.1:1 R:R to the 52-week high.
**What would move it down:** a durable Hormuz de-escalation that collapses Mediterranean cracks, an H1 miss that puts the 2026 estimate out of reach, or any signal that the 120%-of-earnings payout is being funded by running down net cash rather than by a recovering margin. A close back below the 3-touch ₺231.2 shelf would break the structural thesis outright.
