## Bull case
- **The balance sheet is not the problem.** Net cash of ~₺515mn (cash ₺695.6mn vs total debt ₺180.2mn, 31/12/2025) with total liabilities at 31% of equity means MIATK has years of runway to fix the P&L. There is no distress here and no forced-financing event — this is a value question, not a survival question.
- **The sector is a structural winner and MIATK sits in defensible niches** — e-signature, biometrics, public-sector systems, with US and Qatar footholds. Ankara peers ARDYZ (+117.5% YTD) and FONET (+88.5% YTD) prove the demand is real; if MIATK converts even partially, the operational gap to peers is a closable one.
- **Early technical stabilization at a real level.** MACD has crossed bullish with a positive histogram, RSI is recovering from oversold (a mean-reverting signal on BIST per this project's own backtest, not a bearish one), a 3-touch support at ₺30.13 sits directly underneath, and selling volume is drying up (−28.8% vs average). The Lider Sistem merger completing plus a Q2 loss-narrowing in mid-August could power a sharp counter-trend bounce toward ₺36.92.

## Bear case
- **The accounting profit is not cash.** FY2025 operating income of +₺729.9mn against free cash flow of −₺1,283.8mn is a ~₺2.0bn gap, with non-current assets at 61% of the balance sheet. Development spend is being capitalized rather than expensed — which means reported "operating profit" overstates the business, reported book value is ~₺3.4bn of intangibles, and tangible book is only ~₺4.2/share against a ₺30.82 price.
- **It is expensive, not cheap, despite falling 47%.** At EV/Sales 5.9× it carries a ~46% premium to a peer average of ~4.05× in which *every* peer is profitable — while MIATK posts a −39.6% net margin, −18.2% ROE, and real revenue contracting ~30% YoY after inflation adjustment. A −47% drawdown from the high has not made this a value stock; it has only made it a less extreme growth stock without the growth.
- **Narrative is outrunning substance, and the tape agrees.** Quantified contracts in the last quarter total ~₺25mn (≈1% of revenue) while unquantified announcements span nuclear SMRs and Chinese robotaxis; the IR Director resigned on 2026-07-31. Meanwhile the stock is −34.6% *relative* to the index while its two closest Ankara comparables roughly doubled. And SMA50 is collapsing toward SMA200 — a death cross would activate both the freefall penalty and the −7 tape term at the next scan.

## Verdict
MIATK sits in the worst quadrant of the Q/P map: **mediocre quality (Q 44.7) at an unattractive price (P 23.2)** — and unusually, the price axis is the *weaker* of the two, which is rare for a stock down 47% from its high. The rating is driven not by the drawdown but by the arithmetic underneath it: a company losing ₺987mn TTM on real revenue shrinking ~30%, converting +₺730mn of reported operating profit into −₺1.28bn of free cash flow, still asking a 46% EV/Sales premium to four profitable peers. The clean net-cash balance sheet is what separates this from a distressed situation and is the reason the stance is "reduce into strength" rather than anything more urgent — there is no cliff here, only a long re-rating. For existing holders the measured path is trimming into rallies toward the ₺36.92 shelf rather than selling into the ₺30.13 support; for non-holders there is nothing to do, and the 0.76:1 risk/reward on the obvious bounce trade confirms it. **What would change the stance:** Q2 2026 results (~mid-Aug 2026) showing operating cash flow turning positive with the loss narrowing, the Lider Sistem merger closing on disclosed accretive terms, and — the honest precondition — a price closer to the ₺14-19 fair-value band, from which the same business would score very differently. Until cash generation and price meet somewhere in the middle, this is a good sector's weakest listed operator at its highest relative multiple.
