## Bear case
- **It does not earn anything, and the "cheap" book is an accounting artefact.** Q1-2026 net margin was **0.16%** on an 8.6% gross margin; ROE is ~0. The low PD/DD rests on a TMS-29 inflation-restated equity base that simultaneously inflates book and inflates depreciation. A zero-ROE asset base *deserves* to trade below book — cheap can stay cheap indefinitely. This is the textbook value-trap profile.
- **Real revenue is shrinking.** ₺50.2bn (FY25) → ₺59.6bn (FY26E) is +18.6% nominal against ~29-33% inflation = **−10% to −12% real**, and the Q1-2026 run-rate (~₺42bn annualised) is worse than both. Volume growth in the national data is not reaching Kardemir's top line in real terms.
- **Structurally the wrong technology and no upstream integration.** BF-BOF is the carbon-intensive route just as **CBAM** phases in, while scrap-fed EAF competitors are advantaged; Kardemir imports its iron ore and coking coal in USD with no vertical integration; and ~63% national capacity utilisation plus dumped imports means there is no pricing power to pass any of it on.
- **The moment is wrong even if the story is right.** Price is pinned under a 4-touch resistance at ₺41.835 on volume 40% below its 20-day average with MACD freshly crossed bearish, and the first reliable support is 13.5% below. Risk/reward at spot is **0.77:1**.
