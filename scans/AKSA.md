## Block 4 — Connections / value chain (Quality 15%) → **66**

- **Scale moat:** world's **largest acrylic fibre producer**, **330,000 t/yr single-site capacity at Yalova**, ~**19% of the global acrylic fibre market** (AKSA Integrated Report 2025 / Tekstil News). Product range spans acrylic fibre, outdoor fibres, acrylic filament yarns, modacrylics, industrial and recycled fibres.
- **The structural growth leg is carbon fibre.** **Aksa Carbon's consolidation was named as the decisive contributor to the Q1 2026 FAVÖK margin improvement**, and the DowAksa platform feeds wind energy, aerospace, automotive lightweighting and defence composites — end markets growing far faster than textiles.
- **Downstream mix:** apparel/knitwear and home textiles (soft), carpets (Turkish export volumes fell in 2025), outdoor/technical fibres (growing) — the technical leg is what offset the textile contraction in 2025.
- **Geographic diversification with a live geopolitical wrinkle:** exports across ~50 countries; the **Iran channel remained actively used through Q1 2026 despite the war that began in March 2026** — resilience demonstrated, but exposure acknowledged.
- **Upstream:** acrylonitrile/propylene input costs are the main uncontrolled swing factor. Partly mitigated by the **Akkök Holding ecosystem** (Ak-Kim chemicals, Aksa Enerji energy linkage).

**66** — a real moat and a real growth option, tethered to cyclical textile demand and an input cost the company does not set.

Meanwhile leverage is falling (2.54×) and the balance sheet already funded a ₺0.58 dividend.

## Bear case
- **The valuation leg does not support the price.** My base intrinsic value is ₺11.50 (methods A/B/C cluster ₺10.1–10.8) versus ₺13.00 — a **13% premium**, and street consensus of ₺11.78 is *below* the market. At 10.9× TTM FD/FAVÖK and 1.50× book on a ~13% ROE, AKSA is priced as a quality compounder while earning a cyclical's return.
- **Headline earnings are flattered.** FY2025's ₺4.02bn net profit on ₺5.72bn FAVÖK with ₺19bn of net debt at 37% policy rates implies heavy non-operating/monetary contribution. Strip that and normalised EPS is ₺0.80–0.95, putting the shares on ~15× normalised — expensive, not cheap. FY2025 FAVÖK also *fell* (₺5.86bn → ₺5.72bn).
- **Cyclical and leveraged into a soft demand year.** Net debt/FAVÖK 2.54× that the company itself calls high; input acrylonitrile/propylene costs are uncontrolled; Turkish carpet export volumes fell in 2025; the sector's other large caps are impaired (PETKM negative FAVÖK three years running, SASA ₺21.99bn FY2025 loss). Add the March-2026 Iran war touching an active export channel. Price is above the upper Bollinger band, so any Q2 miss on 2026-08-13 hits a crowded, extended position.
